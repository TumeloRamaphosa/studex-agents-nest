# BUILD REPORT — 2026-09-25

## What Was Built

**CIIE 2026 Lead Nurture Engine**
- Location: `/workspace/ciie-nurture/`
- Type: Node.js automation tool (backend script)

A fully automated 3-email nurture sequence for CIIE 2026 outreach, targeting 9 qualified coffee buyers in China and the UAE.

---

## What It Does

The nurture engine sends a structured 3-email sequence to each CIIE prospect:

| Email | Day | Purpose |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Email 1 | Day 0 (now) | Intro + CIIE meeting request + catalog offer |
| Email 2 | Day 3 | Rwanda origin story + PROWTC cert + FOB pricing + sample offer |
| Email 3 | Day 7 | Formal quotation + CIIE booth appointment request |

**Prospects targeted (9):**
- China: Luckin Coffee, Tim Hortons China, Pacific Coffee, JD.com
- UAE: Smart Square, Dhirani Foodstuff, BMC Overseas, Coffee Planet, Third Line Coffee

**Anti-spam safeguards:**
- State tracking per prospect (never double-sends)
- Days-based sequencing (Day 0 → Day 3 → Day 7)
- Dry-run mode (`--dry-run`) before every live run
