**Source:** `/workspace/sa-hospitality-coffee/index.html` (static HTML, no build step)

**What it does**

  | Origin | 1 kg | 5 kg | 20 kg | 30 kg + |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

  | Honey | R350 | R335 | R320 | R310 |
  | Aerobic | R380 | R365 | R350 | R335 |

- **Service lines priced separately** — the part nobody else sells:

  | Line | Price | Unit |
  |---|---:|---|
  | Barista training — foundations | R7,500 | per on-site day, up to 8 staff |
  | Barista training — monthly retainer | R3,500 | per month, 3-month minimum |
  | In-room single-serve sachet | R3.95 | per 2 g sachet, 1,000+ units |
  | Sample pack — 5 × 250 g | R495 | credited against a first 20 kg order |

- **Commercial terms** published up front: 5 kg roasted MOQ, 20 MT green MOQ, roasted-to-order dispatch in 2 business days, free delivery over R850 (Gauteng) / R1,500 (national), 48–72 h delivery, 30-day terms after the first order.
- **A working quote-request flow** — business, contact, business type, monthly volume, coffee checkboxes, service checkboxes, delivery city, notes. It builds a structured message and hands it to **WhatsApp or email** (no backend, no API key, works on a static page), with a copy-to-clipboard fallback.
- **MOQ contradiction fixed.** The page said "Trade MOQ 50 kg" next to *roasted* prices while its green section said 20 MT. 50 kg was never a real order size for either segment. Roasted hospitality MOQ is now **5 kg** — which is also the tier table's own opening rung.

### 1.1 Why the ladder is shallow — and why that is the finding, not a failure

Every published cell clears a **25% margin floor**. The floor is computed from a landed-cost model built on the **top** of the green FOB band, the **higher** of two live FX rates (16.64), plus duty, freight, roasting and packaging explicitly.

Natural at 1 kg (R270) clears a 25% floor by R23.83/kg. At a 30% floor the 30 kg+ tier would need R256.61 — **above the R270 list price before any discount at all**. A 30% floor would forbid the entire volume ladder. So the target is 25%, the ladder is as deep as the cost model allows, and the margin table is published internally so the thin line is visible rather than buried.

**Sensitivity:** at green **$7.50/kg** (an 11% rise on the band high), Natural's 5 kg tier drops to **24.5% — below the floor**. That is the number to watch, and it is why assumption #1 (re-verify the band) is the top owner action.

### 1.2 The generator — one source of truth

`sa-b2b-coffee/compute-price-sheet.mjs` → `sa-b2b-coffee/price-sheet.generated.json`. The published table, the internal cost model, the margins and the sensitivity table are **all emitted from one file**, so the page and the model cannot drift. Change an input, re-run, both move.
