## What It Does

Wildlife Coffee by Studex is a premium B2B coffee supply landing page targeting two channels:

1. **Hotels & Restaurants (HORECA)** — Three product tiers:
   - Wildlife House Blend (R140–160/kg, 10kg MOQ) — Beanco-roasted, your brand or ours
   - Single-Origin Rwanda (R180–220/kg, 5kg MOQ) — Women's cooperative, Rainforest Alliance certified, 86 cupping score
   - Private Label (R160–200/kg, 20kg first order) — Full white-label, Beanco roasts, Studex supplies

2. **Corporate Offices** — Three subscription tiers:
   - Starter: R899/month → 3kg delivered
   - Growth: R1,499/month → 5kg + brewer loaned free
   - Enterprise: R2,499/month → 10kg + full espresso machine loan + custom blend

**Key differentiators:**
- No MOQ on first order (first-mover advantage over every other SA B2B coffee supplier)
- Wagyu biltong welcome pack on every new client order (Studex cross-sell)
- WhatsApp-first ordering (form submits → WhatsApp deep link)
- Beanco white-label partnership highlighted throughout
- 48hr sample delivery promise
