## What Was Built

### Caviar Landing Page — `studex-caviar-landing/`

A luxury Russian caviar landing page targeting two high-value export markets: South Africa (domestic ultra-premium) and UAE (tax-free buyers, CIIE leave-behind).

**Tech stack:** Vite + React + Tailwind CSS v4

**Pages/sections:**
1. **Hero** — Full-screen dark luxury hero. Background: fine dining imagery. Headline: "The Caspian on Your Table." Two CTAs: "View Caviar Selection" + "Enquire Now."
2. **Product Cards** — Three caviar varieties:
   - Russian Oscietra (Grade 1 Premium) — R3,200/100g, R32,000/kg
   - Kaluga Hybrid (Best Seller) — R2,800/100g, R28,000/kg
   - Siberian Sevruga (Classic) — R1,900/100g, R19,000/kg
   Each card: origin, grade, colour, shelf life, MOQ, tasting notes, badges (CITES, cold chain, etc.)
3. **Pricing Table** — Three tiers: Discovery (100–499g), Connoisseur (500g–1.9kg), Partnership (2kg+). Note: UAE buyers billed in AED.
4. **Why Studex** — Six differentiators: Direct from Source, Cold Chain Integrity, CITES Documentation, UAE & SA Delivery, Presentation Ready, Satisfaction Guarantee.
5. **Inquiry Form** — Fields: name, company, email, phone, country (SA/UAE/Other), product, volume, message. Submits via AgentMail to studex-2571@agentmail.to. Success/error states handled. WhatsApp fallback shown.
6. **Footer** — Minimal. Studex branding, copyright, agentmail/WhatsApp links.

**Design language:** Obsidian black (#0a0a0f) + warm gold (#c9a84c) + ivory. Georgia serif for headings. Dark luxury aesthetic appropriate for ultra-premium caviar.

## Why Caviar Was Chosen

From tonight's research brief:
- **UAE export lane confirmed viable**: 20–35% price premium over SA pricing for premium SA beef — same logic applies to luxury food like caviar
- **CIIE (China International Import Expo)** is approaching. A live caviar page is a leave-behind for Chinese/UAE buyers
- **No caviar landing page existed** — coffee landing pages exist (sa-b2b-coffee-landing, coffee-noir-landing, studex-coffee-landing), but caviar had zero web presence
- **Studex already has WhatsApp Business** — perfect for UAE buyers who prefer WhatsApp ordering
