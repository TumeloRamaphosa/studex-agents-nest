**Project:** Studex Caviar — Premium Russian Caviar Landing Page
**Location:** `/workspace/caviar-landing/`

## What It Does

A luxury one-pager targeting South African premium restaurants, hotels, and high-net-worth individuals interested in Russian caviar (Oscietra Gold, Kaluga Royal, Siberian Classic). Features:

- **Hero** — "The Caspian, Delivered to Cape Town" with dark/gold luxury aesthetic
- **Product Cards** — 3 caviar varieties with size/price tables (50g–500g)
- **Trust Bar** — Cold-chain certified, import permits, EUDR compliance, nationwide SA delivery
- **Pricing** — Clear per-size pricing in ZAR, MOQ 500g messaging
- **Inquiry Form** — Name, company, email, quantity dropdown, message — submits in-session (no backend needed for MVP)
- **Mobile responsive** — Full responsive breakpoints
