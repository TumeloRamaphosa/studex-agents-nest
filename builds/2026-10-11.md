## What Was Built

**CIIE 2026 China Coffee Landing Page** — a polished, single-page pitch site for the China International Import Expo.

## Live URL
https://s6msv8j0whmx.space.mcode.io

## What It Does

A dark luxury landing page targeting CIIE 2026 visitors (November 5–10, Shanghai). It sells Rwanda-origin Arabica coffee to Chinese buyers using the headline hook: **Zero Tariff into China**.

### Sections:
1. **Hero** — "The African Coffee Opportunity" with gradient text, CIIE date/location badge
2. **Stats Bar** — 145.7% import surge, $95B market, 0% tariff, 53 countries
3. **Rwanda Origin Story** — altitude, process, grade cards + provenance narrative
4. **Zero Tariff Advantage** — 4-card grid explaining the May 2026 policy + margin opportunity
5. **White-Label Products** — 250g / 500g / 1kg tiered pricing cards (FOB $12–18/kg), MOQ 50kg
6. **Sample Request Form** — Name, Company, Email, Message → in-app confirmation state
7. **UAE/MENA Horizontal** — Secondary market angle for hotel F&B buyers
8. **Footer** — Studex branding + CIIE booth details
