## WHAT WAS BUILT

**Caviar D2C Landing Page** — `studex-war-room/src/components/CaviarPage.tsx`

A premium, full-screen React landing page for the first direct-to-consumer caviar brand in South Africa. Built as a new tab (`CAVIAR`) in the existing Studex War Room Vite app.

---

## WHAT IT DOES

The page covers the full Studex Caviar go-to-market in a single scrollable experience:

### Sections
1. **Hero** — "The World's Rarest. Delivered to South Africa." with floating animated caviar tin visuals, trust badges (CITES compliant, Dubai sourced, cold chain verified), and dual CTA buttons
2. **The Tasting Box** — Featured card for the R1,800 Nov 2026 limited edition gift box (Kaluga 30g + Osetra 30g + Studex biltong 50g + cold brew 100ml), with 4 individual product cards showing what each component is and its standalone price
3. **Pricing Table** — Clear volume pricing for Kaluga, Osetra, and the Tasting Box, with corporate pricing for 5+ and 10+ unit orders
4. **Sourcing Story** — Dubai-to-SA supply chain explained simply: CITES docs, DAFF compliance, cold chain, EFT/BizCheckout payments, and a visual timeline: Order → Dubai dispatch → SA customs → Delivered (5–7 days)
5. **Corporate Inquiry Form** — Name, email, company, product interest dropdown, and message field. Submits via AgentMail API to robusca.romanov@studex-group.com

### Design Language
- Dark luxury aesthetic: `#0d1117` background, `#c9a84c` gold accents, Georgia serif headings
- Animated floating caviar cans in the hero (CSS float animation)
- Full mobile responsiveness
- No Tailwind — pure CSS with custom properties
