## What It Does

A premium e-commerce UI that lets customers build a custom box of Studex products (biltong, wagyu, caviar, coffee):

### Step 1 — Pick (3-5 cuts)
- Product grid with category filtering (All / Biltong / Wagyu / Caviar / Lamb / Coffee)
- 8 real products with images, descriptions, pricing
- Selection counter (0–5 items)
- Stock availability badges (Out of Stock, Premium, Luxury, Best Seller)

### Step 2 — Configure
- Weight sliders + dropdown for each selected product
- One-time vs Monthly subscription toggle
- Running total with live R-price calculation
- Monthly discount (R50 off recurring)

### Step 3 — Checkout
- Lead capture form: Name, Email, WhatsApp, Address, Notes
- Order summary sidebar with Charlie Agent attribution
- Submit → confirmation screen with order recap
- WhatsApp deep-link to Charlie agent

## Design Decisions

- **Dark theme** (charcoal/black/gold) — luxury positioning matching Snake River Farms aesthetic
- **3–5 item minimum** — forces bundle thinking, increases AOV (average order value)
- **WhatsApp-first confirmation** — SA market preference, bypasses email deliverability issues
- **Charlie Agent attribution** — reinforces agentic OS narrative, builds trust
- **No payment capture on first version** — lead capture first, payment via WhatsApp/Charlie
