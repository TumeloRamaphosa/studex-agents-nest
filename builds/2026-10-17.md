The page is Tumelo's B2B sales tool for ACT Expo Kigali — a 3-day specialty coffee + tea trade show in Rwanda expecting 100+ international buyers from 25+ countries.

**Key features:**
1. **EN / 中文 Mandarin language toggle** — top-right nav button, switches entire page to Mandarin for Chinese buyer outreach at expo
2. **ACT Expo Kigali countdown timer** — live countdown to Oct 28, 2026 in hero section
3. **Expo badge + social proof bar** — SA Pavilion / Booth TBC prominently displayed; 100+ international buyers messaging
4. **3-product catalog** with pricing: House Blend (R140-160/kg), Single-Origin Rwanda (R180-220/kg), Private Label (R160-200/kg)
5. **Why International Buyers Choose Us** — 4-card grid: Hotel Groups, Pan-African Supply, Wildlife Story, Welcome Pack
6. **How It Works** — 3-step flow with WhatsApp lead capture
7. **FAQ** — who roasts, contracts, branding, barista training, Africa delivery
8. **Inquiry form** → WhatsApp deep link (wa.me/27794988737) on submission
