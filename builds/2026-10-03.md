## What It Does

Luxury B2B landing page targeting hotels, restaurants, and luxury retailers in South Africa. Key features:

- **Hero Section** — Full-screen dark luxury aesthetic with gold (#c9a84c) accent, hero image overlay, dual CTAs (View Collection / Speak to Us)
- **Trust Bar** — 4 trust signals: Caspian Sea Origin, Cold Chain Certified, SA Customs Cleared, B2B Only
- **Product Collection** — 4 product cards with Unsplash imagery, pricing in ZAR, MOQ display, and "Request Quote" CTA:
  - Studex Sevruga Reserve 30g — R850 (MOQ 10 units)
  - Studex Oscietra Gold 100g — R2,200 (MOQ 5 units)
  - Studex Imperial Gift Tin 250g — R3,500 (MOQ 3 units)
  - Studex Biltong + Caviar Tasting Box — R4,800 combo (MOQ 2 units)
- **Sourcing Section** — Direct Caspian Sea sourcing story with certifications (CITES, cold-chain, SA health certs)
- **Volume Pricing Table** — Trade pricing structure with MOQ breakdown
- **B2B Inquiry Form** — Full lead capture (name, company, email, phone, enquiry type, volume, message) → submits directly to `ceo@agent.studexmeat.com` via AgentMail API
