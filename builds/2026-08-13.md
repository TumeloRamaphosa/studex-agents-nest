---

## What It Does

### Generator Mode
- **Select prospect type:** Hotels (10 prospects), Restaurants/Cafés (10), OCS Providers (4)
- **Select specific prospect:** Each with location, segment, primary sales angle, and notes
- **Choose outreach channel:** Cold Email, Warm Email, LinkedIn DM, WhatsApp
- **Custom angle field:** Override the default angle for that prospect
- **Your details:** Pre-filled with Robusca Romanov / robusca.romanov@studex-group.com
- **Generate:** AI-composed personalised message with [Name] placeholders, product angles, call-to-action
- **Copy to clipboard:** One-click copy
- **Mark as Sent:** Directly tracks outreach status from the generator

### Tracker Mode
- Stats: Not Contacted / Emailed / Replied / Won — live counts
- Full grid of all 24 prospects with status badges
- Click any status button to advance a prospect through the pipeline
