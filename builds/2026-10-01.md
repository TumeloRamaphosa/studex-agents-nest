# Build Report — 2026-10-01

## What Was Built
**Studex Caviar Landing Page** — A full B2B luxury landing page for the Studex Caviar Division. Dark navy/gold theme. Direct competitor to the coffee landing page (2026-09-30) but for the luxury caviar market.

## Live URL
https://zrca547q8rss.space.mcode.io

## What It Does
- **Hero section** — Full-screen dark luxury hero with animated pulse badge "Now Sourcing to South Africa". Products listed: Osetra, Beluga, Sevruga. Direct sourcing message.
- **Stats strip** — 4 product varieties, 250g MOQ, 21-day shelf life, 3–5 day SA DAFF clearance.
- **Products section** — 4 product cards (Osetra Gold R2,400–R4,200/kg, Beluga Royal R5,800–R9,600/kg, Sevruga Imperial R1,800–R3,400/kg, Golden Oscietra R3,600–R6,400/kg). Each with roe colour swatches, certifications, best-for tags, price, and MOQ.
- **Trade pricing table** — FOB USD and landed ZAR per kg, MOQ, shelf life. "Quote" CTA per row.
- **Sourcing section** — 5-step supply chain: Caspian Sea → St. Petersburg → OR Tambo → SA DAFF → last-mile delivery. Visual supply route with cost breakdown.
- **For buyers section** — 4 buyer personas: Fine Dining, Hotel Groups, Private Dining/Clubs, Wholesale Distributors. Each with pain point and how Studex solves it.
- **Live pipeline preview** — Kanban board pulling from the War Room caviar leads (The Saxon Spa, Mount Grace Hotel, RDJ Consulting, Nairobi Yacht Club, Emirates Palace). Shows stage + company + product + volume.
- **FAQ accordion** — 5 questions covering MOQ, SA DAFF import process, cold chain, SA delivery cities, personal/gifting orders.
- **Inquiry form** — Full B2B lead capture: name, company, email, phone, product interest dropdown, volume dropdown, message. Success state with confirmation message.

## Design
- Dark navy (#0A0F1E) + gold (#C9A84C / #D4AF37) luxury theme
- Tailwind CSS, fully responsive
- Animated hero badge with pulse effect
- Product roe colour swatches per species
- Accordion FAQ with smooth open/close

## Why This Was Priority
The War Room already had a Caviar Pipeline tab with live leads. A public-facing landing page converts that pipeline work into a revenue-generating asset — buyers can now self-qualify via the page before reaching out. Pairs with the Coffee landing page (2026-09-30) to give Studex a professional B2B web presence across two high-value verticals.
