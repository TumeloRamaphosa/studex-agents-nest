## What Was Built

**Coffee Noir B2B Landing Page** (`/workspace/coffee-noir-landing/`)

A luxury dark-themed landing page targeting SA hotels, restaurants, offices, and corporate gifting buyers. Positions Rwandan single-origin coffee (Nyaruguru Women's Cooperative) + biltong bundle as the unique Studex offering.

### Pages / Sections
- **Nav bar** — sticky, links to Products / Why Us / Pricing / Contact
- **Hero** — "Rwanda's Finest. Served in SA." with CSS coffee cup visual
- **Social proof bar** — SCA 86+, 340 women, R2/bag to Women's Fund, Sep 2026 shipment
- **Product cards** — Light Roast, Medium Roast, Espresso Blend with MOQ + pricing
- **Biltong + Coffee Corporate Bundle** callout — unique Studex differentiator
- **Why Us** — Verified Provenance, Barista Training Included, Flexible MOQ, Biltong Bundle
- **B2B Pricing Table** — product / MOQ / retail / cost / margin columns
- **Inquiry Form** — company, name, email, phone, volume, venue type, message

### Key Feature: Live Lead Routing
The inquiry form POSTs to **AgentMail API** on submit:
- Sends rich HTML email to `robusca.romanov@studex-group.com`
- Subject: `New B2B Lead: {Company} ({Venue Type})`
- Body includes all form fields + auto-lead banner
- Tumelo gets a real email notification within seconds of any inquiry
