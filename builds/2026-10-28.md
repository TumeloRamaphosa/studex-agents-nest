## 3. LADDER LOCK — price-integrity & caviar-compliance guard ⭐ NEW

**Source:** `/workspace/ops/price-ladder-guard.py` · **Run:** `python3 ops/price-ladder-guard.py`
**Reports:** `builds/reports/ladder-lock-2026-10-28-{before,after}.md`

A guard that re-derives the defects from live sources on every run, so they cannot silently return after someone edits a page. It checks the live Shopify storefront, the local catalog export, and every live customer-facing page.

| Check | Severity |
|---|---|
| Case-vs-singles arbitrage | BLOCKING |
| Small-pack per-kg penalty, same form, over the category limit | BLOCKING |
| Small-pack per-kg penalty, under the limit | WARN |
| Caviar priced + an order path, with no permit stack | BLOCKING |
| Unconditional CITES/ITAC compliance claim | BLOCKING |
| Price drift between catalog export and live storefront | BLOCKING |
| Price above the category market ceiling without a named benchmark | WARN |

**The guard found three real defects beyond the brief's two**, all still open:
- **Coffee ladder inverted** — Aerobic A1 500g is R500/kg against R380/kg for the 1kg (A2: R300 vs R250. A3/A4: R300 vs R200). This is a genuine inversion and I did **not** fix it tonight — eight days to CIIE with the coffee band in play is the wrong time to re-price a graded coffee ladder. It goes on the review queue with the numbers attached.
- **12 beef-cut inversions** — smaller cuts carrying a per-kg premium. Normal SA butcher convention, so the guard holds a 45% limit on beef and a 15% limit on biltong and coffee. Flagged, not mandated.
- **Caviar priced on the caviar page and the hospitality page** — both still publish caviar retail prices. See the owner actions below.

**Three false-positive traps were found and closed while building it**, because a guard that cries wolf gets switched off:
1. **Category** — a biltong market ceiling was being applied to coffee pods and beef steaks. Every rule is now category-scoped, and Shopify's default `product_type` of `"simple"` is inferred from title/handle (without this, live biltong SKUs fell outside the biltong ceiling entirely).
2. **Form** — a 10 × 50g coffee pod (R2,000/kg) was being compared with a 1kg bean bag (R350/kg). Pod-vs-bag is normal merchandising; per-kg comparison now requires the same pack form.
3. **Context** — the "caviar is priced" detector matched *any* price followed by `/ 1kg`, so the biltong price R1,250/1kg read as a caviar price and flagged our own clean page. A caviar price is now tied to a caviar **grade**.

**Before / after (same guard, final rules):**

|---|---|---|
| Caviar sold without a permit stack | 1 page scanned | 3 pages *(2 newly detected — the caviar page and the hospitality page had never been scanned before)* |
| Our own pages with a caviar sale path | retail page | **0** — retail page and price sheet both REVIEW, gifting CLEAN |
| Biltong ladder | inverted + case arbitrage | **fixed in source and on the deployed page** |
