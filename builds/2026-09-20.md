## WHAT WAS BUILT

### SA B2B Coffee Outreach Email Sequencer
**Location:** `/workspace/sa-b2b-coffee-outreach/`

A Node.js script that fires personalised first-touch emails to SA hotels and restaurants via the AgentMail API. Built on tonight's research angle: China opened to all 53 African countries (July 2026), Rwanda coffee FOB at USD 4.80/kg competitive, cold brew is the summer differentiator, barista training converts bean supply into retainer contracts.

---

## WHAT IT DOES

1. **Reads contacts** from `contacts.json` — 10 verified SA targets
2. **Personalises per contact** — hotel angle vs restaurant angle, first-name greeting, property-specific framing
3. **Sends via AgentMail** — uses `robusca.romanov@studex-group.com` (verified inbox)
4. **Tracks state** — `send-state.json` prevents double-sends across runs
5. **Rate-limits** — 1 email every 8 seconds, daily warmup cap (5 emails day 1)
6. **Logs everything** — `send-log.jsonl` for full audit trail
