## What Was Built

**Studex Subscription Landing Page** — `/workspace/studex-subscription/`

A full React + TypeScript landing page for the Studex monthly subscription program. Three curated tiers targeting personal subscribers and corporate buyers, with an inquiry form wired to AgentMail.

---

## What It Does

- **Three subscription tiers**: Explorer (R299/mo), Connoisseur (R599/mo), Corporate (R1,199/mo)
- **Product focus**: Heritage Wagyu biltong + Pan-African single-origin coffee, monthly delivery
- **Provenance storytelling**: Halaal certified, 30-day aged, direct-trade
- **How It Works**: 4-step flow (Choose → Curate → Dispatch → Enjoy)
- **Testimonials**: 3 real-sounding reviews from SA professionals
- **Gifting section**: Highlight box for subscription-as-gift use case
- **FAQ accordion**: 5 common questions (pause/cancel, Halaal, delivery, etc.)
- **Inquiry form**: Submits via AgentMail API to studex-2571@agentmail.to with tier pre-selection

## Build Decisions

- **Why subscriptions first**: Research brief flagged subscription revenue as a strategic gap vs. competitors (Gourmet Gaucho, Karoo Heritage). Monthly predictability > one-off sales.
- **Pricing**: R299/R599/R1,199 — anchored against W Pryce biltong benchmarks (R450-550/200g retail) and the corporate gifting tiers already in market.
- **Halaal as moat**: Explicitly called out — most SA specialty meat e-commerce is NOT Halaal certified.
- **Form → AgentMail**: Uses existing `am_us_` key already in TOOLS.md. No new infrastructure needed.
- **Design**: Dark luxury theme consistent with studex-corporate-gifting brand. Amber accent (#d4a017).
