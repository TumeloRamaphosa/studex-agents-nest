# BUILD REPORT — 2026-09-13

## Build: CIIE 2026 Rwanda Coffee Buyer Brief

### What I Built
A polished, single-page CIIE buyer brief landing page for Rwanda green coffee export. Designed to be embedded in Luckin Coffee + Tim Hortons China outreach emails, printed as a leave-behind at CIIE, and shared as a standalone link.

**Location:** `/workspace/ciie-buyer-brief/`

### What It Does
- Presents Rwanda A1/A2 washed specialty coffee with full product specs
- Highlights China zero-tariff bilateral protocol (effective July 2026)
- Shows FOB pricing for 3 tiers: Sample / Pilot / Volume Contract
- CIIE booth details + meeting booking CTA
- PROWTC + Rainforest Alliance certification badges
- Contact info for Tumelo (CEO) + email links
- Mobile-responsive, print-ready design

### Sections
1. **Hero** — "Africa's Most Precise Coffee Origin" with CIIE dates + dual CTA
2. **Stats Bar** — 869t China exports, $4.80/kg FOB, 87-90+ SCA, 0% tariff
3. **Why Rwanda / Why Now** — 4-card grid on China market opening + terroir
4. **Product Spec Table** — Full A1/A2 technical spec (origin, grade, altitude, cup profile, certs, MOQ, pricing, harvest)
5. **Pricing & Logistics** — 3-tier commercial terms
6. **Studex Advantage** — Why Studex as a supplier vs. commodity
7. **Contact / Meeting Booking** — CIIE booth details + email CTAs

### Tech Stack
- Vite + React + TypeScript
- Cormorant Garamond (headings) + Inter (body) + JetBrains Mono (labels)
- Dark luxury aesthetic: #0a0d14 background, #d4a017 gold accents
- No Tailwind — vanilla CSS-in-JS
