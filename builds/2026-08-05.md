## What It Does

A professional B2B storefront for Rwanda specialty green coffee, targeting:
- **Buyers:** Specialty roasters, hotel groups, HORECA distributors in China, UAE, SA, Europe
- **Product:** Rwanda A1/A2 Washed & Natural green beans — PROWTC certified, FOB Durban

### Pages & Sections
1. **Navbar** — Logo, nav links (Origin, Grades, Pricing, Credentials, CIIE), B2B Inquiry CTA
2. **Hero** — Farm-to-port value prop, key stats (China 0% tariff, 10MT min container, PROWTC)
3. **China Market Banner** — Tariff advantage, market growth stats, CIIE 2026 callout
4. **Origin Section** — Rwanda altitude/cooperative/PROWTC/logistics cards
5. **Grades Section** — 3 product cards: A1 Washed, A2 Washed, A1 Natural (cup scores, MOQ, pricing)
6. **Pricing Table** — Volume-tiered FOB Durban pricing for all 3 grades
7. **Credentials Section** — PROWTC, Rainforest Alliance, UTZ, Fairtrade, GSM, ICO + full doc checklist
8. **CIIE 2026 Section** — Booth confirmed Nov 5–10 Shanghai — meeting booking CTA
9. **B2B Inquiry Modal** — Full form: company, contact, email, phone, country, volume, grade, message
10. **Footer** — Brand, product links, contact

### Key Features
- AgentMail API integration (form → `studex-2571@agentmail.to` on submit)
- Tailwind CSS dark luxury aesthetic
- Fully responsive
- CIIE 2026 booth confirmation badge
- Volume-based pricing table (500kg–10MT+)
