## What Was Built

### 🎯 SA Horeca Coffee Landing Page
**URL:** https://kppk7doecm8b.space.minimax.io

A full B2B landing page targeting South African Horeca buyers — boutique hotels, specialty cafés, gastro restaurants, and corporate offices. The page positions Studex as the direct farm-to-Horeca supplier that cuts out distributors (saving buyers 30–40%) while delivering PROWTC-certified, fully traceable Rwandan and SA specialty coffee.

### What it does
- **Hero section** — PROWTC/Rwanda positioning, dual CTA (sample box + view products)
- **Stats bar** — SA out-of-home coffee market ($43B), 100% traceability, 30–40% distributor savings
- **6-value proposition cards** — Direct farm pricing, PROWTC cert, sample-led sales, Net-30 terms, supply contracts, menu storytelling
- **3-product cards** — Rwanda A1/A2, SA Highlands Blend, Sample Box intro pack with pricing and MOQ
- **Volume pricing table** — 3 tiers (300–499kg / 500–999kg / 1,000kg+ / annual)
- **Testimonials** — 2 fictional but realistic Horeca buyer quotes
- **Inquiry form** — Name, company, email, phone, volume selector, message → success state
