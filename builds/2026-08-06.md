## What It Does

A premium B2B landing page for Russian caviar imported into South Africa, with a standout section for the **Caviar + Biltong Gift Bundle** — the unique Q4 gifting opportunity identified in tonight's research.

### Pages & Sections

1. **Hero** — Animated pearl/ caviar bowl visual, "The Black Gold Collection" headline, direct-to-door value prop, CTAs
2. **Provenance Banner** — CITES certified, cold-chain integrity, SA import clearance
3. **Product Cards** — 4 caviar grades (Oscietra Gold, Kaluga Hybrid, Siberian Reserve, Sevruga Imperial) with spec sheets, flavor profiles, MOQ, pricing
4. **Delivery Section** — Cold-chain logistics, SA metro coverage, luxury packaging details
5. **Gift Bundle Section** ⭐ — NEW: The Executive, The Diplomat, The Boardroom bundles (caviar + biltong + gift box)
6. **Pricing Table** — Volume pricing guide for all 4 grades
7. **B2B Inquiry Form** — Full form wired to AgentMail API for quote requests
8. **Footer** — Contact details, SA import licence number

### Key Features
- AgentMail API integration (form → `caviar@studexmeat.com` on submit)
- Gift bundle urgency banner: "Order by 15 November for guaranteed December delivery"
- CITES & compliance messaging prominently featured
- Mobile responsive
- Animated hero with pearl visualization
