## 1. Caviar Trade Desk — live app ⭐

**URL:** https://awyjxfplygag.space.mcode.io
**Source:** `/workspace/caviar-trade-desk/`
**Stack:** Vite + React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind v4

### What it does

A live, interactive re-implementation of `caviar-landed-cost.xlsx` —
formula-for-formula — with the floor comparison the workbook was missing.

- **Landed cost per SKU**, live: duty, VAT, brokerage, CITES, last-mile, FX.
- **Minimum viable trade price** at a target margin (30% default, adjustable).
- **The flag:** if the current trade price sits below the floor, the SKU card
  turns red and says `Below floor`. This is the thing the workbook never did.
- **Recommended trade price** (floor, rounded up to R100) and **recommended
  D2C retail** (trade + 50% hospitality uplift), with retail per 100g.
- **Copy-to-clipboard trade quote** per SKU, so a quote literally cannot be
  copied out of the app while it is below the floor.
- **CSV export** of the corrected price sheet with every assumption stamped
  into the footer, including which numbers are unverified.
- **Live assumptions panel** — change duty rate, FX, target margin and every
  number updates. Because the duty rate and FX are *unverified assumptions*,
  the tool lets you stress them rather than presenting false precision.
- **SA market bands shown as reference rows** (R8,000–35,000/kg per IndexBox;
  R2,000–6,000 per 100g per local research), so a retail price above the band
  reads as a *deliberate premium play*, not an accident.

### Also corrected — a misreading in the research brief

The brief described the catalogue as carrying "cost/price pairs (ribeye 1kg
R1,950→R2,939)". That is wrong, and it matters:

- `studex-products-catalog.csv` has **no cost column at all**.
- `Price (ZAR)` is the **storefront price** — confirmed: the live store sells
  Luxury Wagyu Biltong 1kg at R650, which is exactly the `Price` column value.
- `Compare At` is the **pre-discount anchor** (a 20% markdown), not cost.

So R1,950→R2,939 is a storefront-price → anchor pair. Nobody should build margin
on it. This is now flagged in the subscription SKU sheet.
