### 2. Caviar lead time corrected — DEPLOYED ✅

**What was wrong:** the page said *"Lead time: 7–14 days from order confirmation."* It did not mention that a **DAFF veterinary import permit** for fish products must be **applied for at least 4 weeks in advance** and takes **5–10 working days** to issue once approved. 7–14 days is the **commercial handling** leg, not order-to-door. To a hotel buying for a Saturday function, that difference is the whole purchase.

**What it says now:**
> Lead time: 7–14 days' commercial handling from order confirmation, counted from the date the DAFF veterinary import permit is issued. For a first shipment the permit must be applied for at least 4 weeks ahead and takes 5–10 working days to issue once approved — allow roughly two months end to end.

The DAFF permit is now a **listed feature**, not a footnote. And the unsourced **"duty (±20%)"** is gone — SA duty on prepared caviar is HS 1604.14.10 = 25% or 200c/kg and our roe-vs-caviar classification is still an open broker question, so the page now quotes **no number**: *"import duty and VAT quoted in the landed cost."*
