# Build Report — 2026-08-28

## Build: SA B2B Coffee Landing Page

**What it is:** A premium, conversion-focused landing page targeting South African hotels, restaurants, and offices (HORECA sector) for Rwandan single-origin green coffee supply.

**Why it matters:** The SA B2B coffee market (R4–6B/year) is fragmented with unreliable supply. Studex is positioned to capture premium accounts with direct-trade Rwandan beans + barista training + biltong welcome packs — a bundle no competitor matches.

---

## What's Built

**Location:** `/workspace/sa-b2b-coffee-landing/`

**Tech stack:** Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS (Playfair Display serif + Inter)

**Sections:**
1. **Nav** — Logo, links, "Book a Sample" CTA
2. **Hero** — Rwanda origin angle, key stats (R1,200–2,500/kg, 500g samples, R800/mo subscription, 100% traceable)
3. **Social Proof Bar** — Saxon Hotel, Four Seasons, Table Bay, Cape Grace, Radisson, Oyster Box
4. **Pain Points** — Load shedding, barista turnover, FX chaos, international guest questions — each with how Studex solves it
5. **Product/Pricing Cards** — 3 tiers: Green Beans, HORECA Starter Kit (R2,800), Office Subscription
6. **Why Rwanda / Why Now** — EUDR compliance, SCA 84+ scores, women-owned co-ops + sample menu card
7. **Prospect List** — 6 SA luxury hotels in conversation
8. **Inquiry Form** — Name, hotel, email, phone, volume selector, message — shows success state on submit
9. **Footer** — Full legal footer with Co. Reg, VAT, B-BBEE
