### What It Does

The script runs daily and gradually builds sender reputation for:
- `robusca.romanov@studex-group.com` — CEO inbox (CIIE, Dubai, China outreach)
- `naledi.cmo@studex-group.com` — CMO inbox (media, content)
- `studex-2571@agentmail.to` — General StudEx inbox

**How warmup works:**
1. Sends 2-10 varied emails/day from each inbox (ramping over 4 weeks)
2. Targets controlled external addresses (Gmail/Outlook of known contacts)
3. Uses rotating subject lines and body templates — no spam patterns
4. Adds tracking pixels to measure deliverability
5. Tracks state to never double-send on the same day
6. Logs everything with timestamps
