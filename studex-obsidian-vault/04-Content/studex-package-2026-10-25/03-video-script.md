# 03 — "StudEx Trade" · 25-second video script + visual brief

**Audience:** hospitality buyer (hotel / restaurant / café / lodge buyer) in South Africa or Kigali.
**Goal:** one argument, about terms — not a product catalogue.
**Primary format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 (Reels / TikTok / WhatsApp Status / LinkedIn).
**Runtime:** **25.0 seconds exactly** (00:00 → 0:25) — inside the 20–30s window.
**Shots:** 9.
**Spoken VO:** **77 words** (≈ 3.1 words/second across 25s).

## The single argument (everything else serves this)

> **Stocking StudEx is a planned supply line, not a gamble: the trade price, the minimum order and the lead time are all stated plainly, so you can put it in a costed menu before you commit a cent.**

The VO therefore talks in **trade terms** — price basis, minimum order, lead time — and only
names a product in order to price it. It never becomes a catalogue. **No landed cost, farm gate,
margin, duty, VAT or FX assumption is stated or implied** — retail and trade terms only.

**The single CTA (on screen and spoken, nowhere else):** `sales@studex-group.com`
One contact only. No second CTA, no phone number, no link, no hashtag stack in the CTA frame.

## 2. Visual brief

### Global style block

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Grade** | Low-key cinematic. Deep lifted-but-crushed blacks (no crushed detail in the product). Warm gold key light, shadows carrying a faint emerald bias. Never teal-and-orange. No artificial HDR halos, no "AI sheen". |
| **Lens / texture feel** | 35mm and 50mm for the human shots (shallow but not creamy), 85–100mm macro for product (S2, S3, S4, S6) with real specular detail on caviar pearls and biltong fat. Slight anamorphic-ish horizontal flare only on the S9 gold hairline. Matte surfaces read as matte — no plastic gloss. Natural motion blur, no slow-motion unless it earns the beat. |
| **Aspect — primary** | **9:16, 1080×1920.** All framing built vertical-first: subject centred in the middle 60% of the frame height, text blocks in the lower-middle band. |
| **Aspect — 1:1 cut-down** | **1080×1080.** Do **not** scale-and-crop from 9:16. Re-frame per shot: keep the hero on the centre axis and re-lay the text block. S2/S3/S4 macro shots reframe to a square crop that keeps the product on the third. S1 and S8 need a genuine re-shoot/reframe with a wider vertical composition. |
| **Aspect — 16:9 cut-down** | **1920×1080.** Built for LinkedIn and the trade page. Re-frame wide, move the text block to the left third, and let the product breathe on the right. S5, S6, S7 were composed with headroom for this and need no re-shoot; S1, S2, S3, S9 do need a dedicated wide pass or a re-shoot with the same props and lighting. |
| **Music / SFX direction** | One bed, low tempo, restrained: deep sub pulse, muted plucked/marimba figure, light shaker — modern African-commercial warmth, **not** tourist-postcard percussion, no cowbell cliché, no "world music" styling. Product SFX sit forward and are mixed above the bed: blade-on-board, foil/tin lift, dry bean pour, paper shuffle, crate latch, stamp thud. S1 opens on **silence + room tone only** (the hook must land in the noise). S6 thins the bed to near-silence under the cold-room hum. Bed resolves fully on the final gold tag. |
| **Type** | One sans (geometric or neo-grotesque). Sentence case for headlines, bold weight only. Prices always in figures, never spelled out on screen. Numbers in the brand Gold, always on Circuit Black or emerald-tinted black for contrast. |
| **Colour discipline** | Emerald `#0B4F3C`, Gold `#C9A84C`, Circuit Black `#0A0A0A` only, plus the natural colour of the product (meat brown, caviar grey-gold, green bean olive). No fourth brand colour; no bright cyan/teal, no red sale badges. |

### Per-shot visual brief

**S1 — The hook** (00:00–00:02)
- **Setting:** boutique hotel bar / restaurant pass, mid-service, Cape Town or Kigali. Background bar shelves out of focus.
- **Subject & action:** hands slicing dark biltong on a worn wooden board; one slice falls away from the blade.
- **Shot size:** detail-to-medium; 50mm, tight on hands and blade, face out of frame.
- **Lighting:** warm practical key from above-right, deep shadow left; gold specular on the blade edge.
- **Palette:** Emerald — bar mat and apron edge; Gold — key-light glint and brass rail glint; Circuit Black — bar body and background falloff.
- **Props:** biltong on board, chef's knife, bar mat, folded apron.
- **Must NOT appear:** no logos on the apron, no restaurant name or street signage, no identifiable customer face, no price tag, no alcohol brand, no award sticker, no "sale/special/cheap" language anywhere in frame.

**S2 — Biltong trade price** (00:02–00:05)
- **Setting:** a trade sample table or the same bar's back counter — dark slate, single overhead pool of light.
- **Subject & action:** macro on craft biltong slices fanned on black slate; a hand lifts one slice toward lens; the emerald packaging band slides into frame behind it.
- **Shot size:** macro (85–100mm), very shallow focus on the lifted slice.
- **Lighting:** hard top light with gold rim on the fat marbling; background drops to black.
- **Palette:** Emerald — packaging band and the faint cool cast in the slate shadow; Gold — rim light on the marbling; Circuit Black — slate surface and full background.
- **Props:** craft biltong slices, black slate, emerald packaging band (unprinted or generic), serving tweezers.
- **Must NOT appear:** no retail shelf-tag or "was/now" price, no RRP sticker, no pack shot of a subscription box, no Stud-Bot product, no discount language.

**S3 — Caviar, retail and trade** (00:05–00:08)
- **Setting:** a private-dining or chef's table surface, black lacquer, a glass of water at the edge of frame.
- **Subject & action:** macro of an open caviar tin; a gold spoon lifts four or five pearls and holds. A **tier card** wipes in from the right over the top of the shot.
- **Tier card content (graphic, not shot text):** three rows, each "Oscietra Imperial — R4,200/100g · R34,000/kg", "Kaluga Prestige — R2,800/100g · R22,000/kg", "Sevtruga Medium — R1,600/100g · R12,500/kg", plus a footer line "Private-label tins from 5 kg MOQ". Card is Circuit Black with a gold hairline rule and gold numerals.
- **Shot size:** macro on tin and spoon; card animates over the top-right third.
- **Lighting:** a single narrow source raking across the pearls so each catches a separate specular; rest of the frame near-black.
- **Palette:** Emerald — a faint emerald wash on the lacquer and the tin's label band; Gold — the spoon, the pearls' highlights, the card's hairline; Circuit Black — lacquer and card ground.
- **Props:** caviar tin, gold spoon, black lacquer surface, chilled glass, the tier card (motion graphic).
- **Must NOT appear:** no vodka bottle, no branded blini or garnish packaging, no client name on a place card, no claim of origin certification, no "Siberian Reserve" or "Sevruga Premium", no "per 250g".

**S4 — Green coffee** (00:08–00:11)
- **Setting:** cupping lab / roastery bench in Kigali or on a South African roastery floor — worn timber bench, black backdrop.
- **Subject & action:** green beans tipped from a hessian sack into a scoring bowl; a scorer's cup, spoon and a printed cupping form on the bench (form text illegible / defocused).
- **Shot size:** medium-wide bench scene, 50mm; one cut to a macro of the bean stream if the timing allows inside the 3.0s.
- **Lighting:** raking side light (window or single hard source) so the beans' green-olive reads; deep shadow on the bench.
- **Palette:** Emerald — the sack stencil and the emerald-tinted shadow in the beans; Gold — a hard gold key catching the bean edges and the brass of the cup; Circuit Black — backdrop, bench shadow side.
- **Props:** hessian sack, green beans, scoring bowl, scorer's cup, spoon, cupping form (blurred).
- **Must NOT appear:** no roastery brand other than StudEx, no cupping scorecard with legible scores (83–87 etc. must not be shown as a number on a card), no country-of-origin bag labelling that contradicts the FOB basis, no USD→ZAR conversion, no port or customs imagery that implies a landed price.

**S5 — Minimum order** (00:11–00:14)
- **Setting:** a trade desk / stockroom counter, back end of a hospitality store.
- **Subject & action:** over-the-shoulder on a printed order sheet and a tablet showing three order lines (shapes only, no readable data); a warehouse case of biltong blurred on racking behind.
- **Shot size:** medium over-shoulder, 35mm; focus pulls from the tablet to the paper sheet.
- **Lighting:** flat, clean overhead light — the only "office" lighting in the film, which makes it feel procedural and honest.
- **Palette:** Emerald — the racking and a clipboard edge; Gold — the clipboard clip, a roll of label stock, and the gold numerals on screen; Circuit Black — the tablet body, the screen ground, the deep racking shadow.
- **Props:** clipboard, order sheet, tablet, biltong case, shelf label roll.
- **Must NOT appear:** no client name, no buyer email, no RFQ number, no internal cost model or margin sheet, no delivery address, no visible contract or PO with data.

**S6 — The caviar lead time** (00:14–00:17)
- **Setting:** cold room or chilled loading dock.
- **Subject & action:** gloved hand latching an insulated crate; a wall-mounted temperature gauge in shot; condensation on the door frame.
- **Shot size:** macro-to-medium; 50mm wide of the cold room, cut to a 100mm detail of the latch and gauge.
- **Lighting:** cold overhead fluorescent — deliberately the only cold-lit shot in the film, so "cold chain" is felt, not stated.
- **Palette:** Emerald — a green-cyan cast in the cold-room light and the crate label; Gold — a warm glint on the gauge dial and the latch hardware; Circuit Black — the insulated interior and the crate's shadow side.
- **Props:** insulated crate, temperature gauge, gloves, condensation.
- **Must NOT appear:** no shipment tracking number, no consignee, no airway bill, no visible certificate text, no clock implying a promised delivery date, no temperature reading that could be read as a guarantee.

**S7 — First shipment reality** (00:17–00:20)
- **Setting:** a black desk in a back office.
- **Subject & action:** a hand brings down an official rubber stamp onto a permit folder; a desk calendar edge behind shows a marked period.
- **Shot size:** medium-detail, 50mm, camera slightly low so the stamp reads as weight.
- **Lighting:** single hard top light; the stamp throw is fast and the shadow lands hard.
- **Palette:** Emerald — the stamp's ink impression and the calendar's spine; Gold — the stamp's brass handle and the marked period; Circuit Black — desk surface and background.
- **Props:** permit folder, rubber stamp, desk calendar.
- **Must NOT appear:** **no readable permit text, no permit number, no agency names, no signature, no date that could be read as a live document.** Use a prop/dummy document or a shot where the page is angled away from the lens. No "booth confirmed"-style certification language, no SABS/DAFF logo as an endorsement badge.

**S8 — Trade pricing on request** (00:20–00:22)
- **Setting:** a hotel office or the buyer's own desk — the room the buyer is sitting in, which quietly hands the perspective to them.
- **Subject & action:** over-shoulder of a buyer at a laptop, a hand on the trackpad, a cup of filter coffee in the other hand. The spreadsheet shape is visible but fully blurred.
- **Shot size:** medium over-shoulder, 35–50mm.
- **Lighting:** window light from the side, slightly blown — the brightest frame in the film, which reads as "the buyer's world".
- **Palette:** Emerald — a soft emerald bounce from a chair or window frame, and the laptop UI's only accent; Gold — the rim on the cup and the trackpad highlight; Circuit Black — the laptop body and the dark foreground.
- **Props:** laptop, coffee cup, notebook, hand.
- **Must NOT appear:** **no readable spreadsheet, no prices, no customer names, no email inbox, no WhatsApp thread**, no screen content sharp enough to OCR. Blur must be true defocus, not a low-res copy.

**S9 — CTA end card** (00:22–00:25)
- **Setting:** a still-life on Circuit Black — the three categories as a family, not as a catalogue shot.
- **Subject & action:** biltong slices, an open caviar tin, and a scoop of green beans arranged with negative space on the right where the end card sits. The end card resolves: wordmark, the line "Trade enquiries", the email, and the tagline.
- **End card content:** **StudEx Trade** wordmark · **Trade enquiries** · **sales@studex-group.com** · tagline **Technology. Capital. Opportunity.**
- **Shot size:** still, composed wide enough to survive the 1:1 and 16:9 cut-downs (subject left-of-centre).
- **Lighting:** three-point product light, emerald wash from below-left, gold rim from upper-right.
- **Palette:** Emerald — the full-bleed wash panel behind the end card and a green cast on the black base; Gold — hairline rule, email address, tagline; Circuit Black — the base field and card ground.
- **Props:** biltong, caviar tin + spoon, green beans, hessian, black surface, end-card graphic.
- **Must NOT appear:** no second contact detail (no phone, no second email, no link, no QR code), no hashtag stack, no "book now"/"buy now", no awards, no address, no logo lockup other than StudEx, no competitor marks.
