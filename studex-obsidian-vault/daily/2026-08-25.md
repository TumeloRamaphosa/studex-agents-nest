### Topic 1: 🇿🇦 SA B2B Coffee — HORECA Landscape

**Key findings:**
- B2B channels = ~60% of global coffee market; HORECA is the core segment
- SA is a **net importer** of coffee → FX risk, global price exposure
- Specialty Coffee market: $37.36B in 2026 → $52.06B by 2031 (6.86% CAGR)
- SA at-home market still dominated by chicory/instant — HORECA premium opportunity is real but needs education

**Critical SA HORECA pain points (from complaints):**
- **Load shedding**: Espresso machines die mid-service; hotels need generator backup = added cost
- **Skilled barista retention**: High turnover, undervalued; training is a recurring cost nobody wants to absorb
- **Supply chain inconsistency**: Green coffee price volatility + rand weakness = menu pricing chaos
- **No brand story**: Generic suppliers offer no provenance narrative — nothing to put on menus
- **Sustainability compliance**: EUDR-aligned, traceable sourcing pressure from international guests

### Topic 2: 🔊 Voicebox — Tumelo Voice Clone on Orgo VM

**What Voicebox is:**
- Open-source, local-first AI voice studio (jamiepine/voicebox on GitHub)
- Free alternative to ElevenLabs / WisprFlow — runs entirely on own hardware
- **Voice cloning: just 3 seconds of reference audio**
- 7 TTS engines built in (Qwen3-TTS, Chatterbox Multilingual, etc.)
- 23 languages supported including Chinese and Arabic
- MCP and ACP/A2A endpoints — plugs directly into OpenClaw
- REST API mode: turn into a local API server with zero rate limits, zero cloud costs

**Best deployment path for Studex:**
- Self-host server mode on **Orgo VM** (Ubuntu, 2vCPU, 8GB RAM)
