### 🛠️ The empty-email defect is now FIXED, and the detector found a THIRD victim (23:00 UTC)

**Detector shipped:** `scripts/check-blank-sends.py` — sweeps all 10 inboxes and flags any message we sent with **size < 600 bytes**.

**How to read the AgentMail API (this is easy to get wrong, and I got it wrong first):**
- The **list** endpoint does **not** populate `text` at all — it is absent for **every** message, including ones with real bodies. “`text` is empty” is **not** evidence of a blank email.
- `preview` is populated inconsistently. Also unreliable.
- **`size` is the only reliable discriminator.** A real message with a body is **~1200–32000 bytes**; a subject-and-headers-only message is **~370–430 bytes**.
- The **detail** endpoint `GET /v0/inboxes/{id}/messages/{message_id}` returns **HTTP 400** — it does not work. Use the list.
🔑 **I first ran a `text`-based sweep and got a false positive list of ~150 “blank” sends, which were mostly real emails to Tumelo.** The `size` rule replaces it. **The detector now reports the truth: 3 external, 5 internal.**

**Result — the defect hit THREE real people, not two:**

**Guarded sender shipped:** `scripts/send-email.py` — now the **only** sanctioned way to send an external email. It:
1. **Refuses an empty body** (validated pre-flight; refuses < 40 chars as a truncation bug)
2. **Reads the body from a file**, so a shell-quoting bug cannot blank it
