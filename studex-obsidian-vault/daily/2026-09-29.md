## 📊 Research Brief — Tonight's Findings (Research Hour)

### Topic 1: SA B2B Coffee Market — HORECA Opportunities

**Key insights:**
- SA specialty coffee market is growing. HORECA operators actively seeking differentiated suppliers.
- Sustainability and provenance story = the two levers buyers care about most
- White-label bean supply = fastest foot-in-door ( cafés like Bootlegger, Origin, Coffee Lab buy roasted beans, not roast their own)
- Hotels = high volume but slow procurement cycle. Entry point: managed coffee service with barista training SLA (not just beans)
- OCS players (Dish Café, Vitago) = competitors AND potential partners — they have machine networks, no barista training or cold brew
- Cold brew = trending, highest margin. RTD SA market projected $226M by 2033

**Top opportunities:**
1. Cold brew concentrate white-label to hotels/restaurants — 5L bags, refill model, price at 3× COGS (target R180–R250/L wholesale)
2. OCS partnership — approach Dish Café / Vitago / Caffè Latto with bean supply + training bundle
3. Drive traffic to existing B2B landing page (`/workspace/sa-b2b-coffee-landing`) via cold outreach + LinkedIn ads
