## 📡 Research Findings (Sunday — 20:00 SAST)

### Topic 1: African Coffee Export Markets — UAE, China, Europe

**UAE:** Dubai is Africa's biggest re-export hub — duty-free, world-class logistics. Premium African beans (Ethiopian Sidamo, Kenyan AA) command $3–8/lb FOB. Buyers = trading houses (Al Ghurair, Al Maya), HORECA importers, specialty roasters.

**China:** Zero-tariff policy (May 2025) for 53 African diplomatic partners = Ethiopian/Kenyan/Tanzanian beans tariff-free. Starbucks China has 7,000+ stores competing for premium Arabica. China's middle class = single biggest growth opportunity for African coffee over the next decade.

**Europe:** Germany (Hamburg) = world's largest green coffee import hub but re-exports roasted/ground. Italy (Trieste) dominates specialty espresso trade. Europeans pay premium for certification (Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade). Nordic roasters driving single-origin premium trends.

**Prices:** Specialty African Arabica fetching $3–8/lb FOB vs $1.40 commodity Robusta. Ethiopia's FX crisis = push to sell faster = price opportunity for buyers. ICO composite up 40%+ since 2023.

**SA as gateway?** No significant SA coffee export — we import everything. But SA could be a roasting/packaging hub for African-origin beans to China — but needs green bean sourcing agreements.

**Opportunities for Studex:**
- Source Ethiopian/Kenyan green beans → roast locally → SA B2B + China OEM channel
- China zero-tariff is a structural tailwind — real opportunity if SA can compete on freight
- UAE HORECA market reachable via existing trade lanes — faster test market
