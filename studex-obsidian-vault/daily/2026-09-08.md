### Topic 2: SA B2B Coffee — HORECA Landscape

**Market:** R12bn annually | HORECA = 55% of market | CAGR ~6.2%

**How buyers procure:**
- Ultra-luxury hotels (Saxon, Ellerman, One&Only): RFP/tender process, 3-6 months. Rainforest Alliance cert required.
- Business hotels (Radisson, Hyatt): Procurement depts, 1-3 months. Volume + consistency + training.
- Boutique cafes (Bootlegger, Origin, Coffee Lab): Direct/relationship, weeks. White-label + story + cold brew.
- OCS providers (Dish Café, Vitago): Partnership model. Don't compete — become their premium bean supplier.

**Top 5 buyer pain points:**
1. Supply consistency — "Can you deliver 50kg/month every month without fail?" #1 filter
2. Certification — Rainforest Alliance now required by international luxury brands
3. Delivery reliability — Cold chain and lead time are a SA logistics nightmare
4. Barista quality — Training is a value-add, not a nice-to-have
5. Price pressure — Coffee inflation real, buyers squeezing

**Wedding season opportunity (Sept-Nov):** Cold brew + biltong event bundles. Cape Town wedding season is NOW.
