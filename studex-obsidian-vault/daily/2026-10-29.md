### 🔴 Lead finding of the night
**We are planning a caviar business on a 30 % import duty and a 48-tonne Russian supply base, while South Africa is the one country in Africa that already imports caviar — and we are not the ones importing it.**

1. **HS 1604.31 (prepared/preserved caviar) carries 30 % import duty + 15 % VAT in SA** (SARS 2026, via JLog). This is the number that moves the R12 500 / R22 000 / R34 000 trade prices. ⚠️ **`caviar-landed-cost.xlsx` was NOT opened — the duty is FOUND, not MODELLED.**
2. **Russian caviar is a rounding error, not a supply line.** Russian farmed sturgeon caviar: **45 t (Jan–Jul 2026, +5 % y/y) · 48 t (Jan–Aug 2026) · 89 t (full-year 2025)** (Shev / Tridge). **China shipped USD 118.2 m = 42.1 % of the global black-caviar trade and produced an estimated 260 t of farmed caviar** (Dataintelo / Mordor) — Chinese scale is what has **lowered global wholesale prices**. Russia→China seafood export revenue **+54 % in the first 9 months of 2026** (TASS) — Russian caviar is being sold into China, not waiting for us.
3. **The trade flow runs the other way from our ambition.** Africa's caviar data: **imports 18 t (USD 935 k) led by South Africa; exports 17 t (USD 4.6 m) from African producers.** ⇒ Our 2027 proposition must be **import-and-distribute**, not "we make caviar in Africa". (⚠️ The Africa $ figures are an indexbox aggregation of a broad caviar-and-sturgeon category and do not reconcile with premium caviar pricing — **do not quote them as price benchmarks.** The directional fact is the usable part.)
