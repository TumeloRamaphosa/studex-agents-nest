## 📡 RESEARCH BRIEF (Research Hour — 10:00 PM SAST)

**Topic 1: Caviar Import — SA Regulations & Tariffs**

**Key Findings:**
- CITES is mandatory for ALL sturgeon caviar (wild AND farmed). Every tin needs a non-reusable label. Importing without CITES = seizure + fines.
- SA requires DFFE import permits (Department of Forestry, Fisheries & the Environment) — separate from CITES, both needed
- Russia-SA trade growing (+16.2%/yr) but dominated by wheat/fertilizers — caviar not yet flowing at commercial scale
- Lab-grown caviar emerging — regulatory framework unclear in SA but worth watching as tariff-free entry point

**Moat Opportunity:** CITES + DFFE compliance, if solved, becomes a genuine moat. First-mover in SA with documented compliance = trust badge competitors can't replicate.

**Recommended Actions:**
1. Hire a customs broker who has done CITES + DFFE for seafood before placing first order
2. Source from CITES-compliant farms first — Swiss/Chinese sturgeon farms with clean documentation
3. Add compliance trust badge on caviar landing page
4. Price in permit costs: CITES admin ~$200-500/batch + DFFE permit ~R800-1500
