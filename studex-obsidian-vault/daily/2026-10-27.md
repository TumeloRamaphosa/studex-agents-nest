### 🟠 Second finding — the SA Horeca coffee sell sheet was selling the wrong thing
- Sell sheet quoted **R130/kg green FOB** to SA hotels. Green FOB is an **export** price.
  A SA hotel buys **landed domestic** coffee. This alone would have failed the first buyer conversation.
- The sheet was pushing **"gourmet grade"** — forbidden copy, no source doc, and reserved for
  **export** buyers only.
- The sheet quoted **both** R95/kg and R75/kg with no explanation — a self-undercut.
- Segment framing was wrong: SA hospitality coffee is a **relationship/wholesale** buyer, not a
  "café or restaurant ordering small quantities" buyer.

### 🟡 Third finding — the addressable market in SA is narrow
- **15 roasters, ~200–300kg/mo combined.** At our (correct) landed cost the whole SA market is
  roughly **R680k–R1.02m/yr**. It is a **credibility-building and price-anchor market, not a volume market.**
- **KZN demand is real** and near us; it was mis-filed as a "gap" from Western Cape focus.
- **Europe/Germany is not a lead** — it is the same EUDR compliance blocker wearing a German
  accent. Do not sell into it.
- **Coffee picking:** mechanical harvest → berries damaged → fermentation defects → no specialty
  grade. Manual harvest only.

### 🟢 Fourth finding — the certifications we could actually claim
- **Prowtc (PROWTC / "Prowtc")** — washed Arabica and Robusta from Rwanda, full traceability
  and cupping records. **Subject to a live contradiction**: MEMORY calls it our primary

- **Rainforest Alliance — NOT held.** No source document, no plot geolocation.

- **Higgsfield** — a gift box platform for EUDR-ready traceable coffee. **Do NOT buy — a share
