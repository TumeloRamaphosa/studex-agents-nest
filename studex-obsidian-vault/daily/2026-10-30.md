## 4. RESEARCH FINDINGS (Research Hour, 22:00 SAST) — `research/2026-10-30.md`

**Lead finding:** we were about to walk into CIIE with **three different green-coffee FOB bands live at once**,
and the one our cost model uses is **priced above the ceiling of the market we are going to quote into**
(see §3, Build 1).

**Topic 1 — who is actually buying African coffee now.**
- **China is fully open**, and it opened in two steps: **1 May 2026** China eliminated tariffs on all taxable
  products from **53 African countries**; **20 July 2026** a single continent-wide approval regime lets
  qualified coffee beans from all 53 countries in (Barista Magazine, CGTN). **No country-by-country fight.**
- **China imported $246m of African coffee in 2025, up from $157m (+57%)** (China-Africa Business Council);
  total China coffee imports $837m (USDA GAIN, Jan 2026). **Shanghai Port handles 28% of China's green-bean
  imports; Kunshan produces ~60% of its roasted coffee** (USDA GAIN, Aug 2026) — the buyer we want is within
  a truck of Kunshan.
- 🟡 **The competition for the Rwanda lane is not other exporters — it is Rwanda's own government channel and
  one Chinese roaster.** Rwanda signed an MoU with **Cotti Coffee** (CIIE, Jun 2025). **Mauritius makes its
  CIIE debut (5–10 Nov) with a China-first story** (50,651 bags / 3,039 t already exported to China) — a
  country with a *coherent national China narrative* arriving at the same show we are. **Ethiopia is the volume
  wall** (470,000 t / $2.65bn in 2024/25; 48,000 t to China in FY2025/26).
  💡 *We are not competing with Ethiopia on tonnage. We are competing on a **5 MT custom-blend lot with published
  cupping and lot traceability** — a size Ethiopia cannot service.*
- 🟢 **The Gulf is a re-export hub, not just a buyer** — and the cheapest gate into Asia. Dubai is the
  transhipment node into MENA; **World of Coffee Dubai 2026** is the Middle East's premier specialty platform.
  MEA coffee market $17.14bn (2026) → $24.89bn (2031), 7.75% CAGR.
- 🟡 **Europe is a price/differentiation market, not a volume market.** CBI: *"The European coffee market is
  saturated."* **EUDR (large/medium 30 Dec 2026)** is a paperwork cost to be treated as a **filter**, not a market.
- 🟡 **The whole trade is price-led, so the differential IS the product.** Global coffee export **value +39%
  in 2025 while volume fell 2.4%** — value up on price, not tonnage. Green is priced as **ICE "C" + a
  differential** for grade, screen size, certification and origin. Our published **cupping score (A1 17+ / 84+;
  A2 15–16)** *is* the differential, and it is the one asset a commodity exporter cannot fake. Our A1 band
  ($4,200–4,800/MT) vs A2 ($3,600–4,000/MT) is a **$600–1,000/MT gap — that gap IS the cupping score being
  monetised**, and it must be stated as such on the CIIE card.

**Topic 2 — premium biltong: what competitors do differently.**
- 🔴 **We are priced well above the SA market.** Food Lover's Market **R249.99/kg** (promo, nationwide) ·
  thatbiltongshop.co.za R300/kg · omgbiltong.co.za from R353 · Hydr8 JHB R375–450/kg · Biltong Boytjies
  R349.99–449.99/kg · Mooiplaats Wagyu R600/kg · Woolworths sliced 400g **R700/kg** (premium supermarket
  ceiling). **Our "budget" tier is R900/kg and our "premium" tier is R1,450/kg, against a market that ends at
  R700/kg.** Every one of those is a trade-buyer objection we cannot answer with a product story.
- 🔴 **Competitors win on *format* and *mechanics*, not on meat.** **Brooklyn Biltong: made in the US, "no
  import"** — they manufacture locally to dodge freight and duty, sell **$13.31 per 3.2 oz bag (~$110–130/kg)**
  with a **subscription** ($33.96). **Ember:** a bite-sized "shot" format — biltong reformatted for the snack
  occasion, not the protein occasion. **Lowfeld Soul Food:** SA-run, made to order, shipped within days —
  *freshness as the moat*.
- 🔴 **Competitors publish our comparison pages.** `btbiltong.com`, `yebobiltong.com`, `brooklynbiltong.com`,
  `jerkybrands.com` all run *"best biltong brands in the US (2026)"* and *"vs other online brands"* content.
  🔑 *They are running our SEO. We have no equivalent content and no comparator page.*
- 🟢 **The Western press is doing our PR for free:** **The Economist, 23 Sep 2026 — "Biltong is going
