## 🔬 Research Brief — 2026-09-13

**Topic:** Luxury Meat E-Commerce (SA competitive landscape) + African Coffee Markets (UAE/China/Europe)

### Luxury Meat E-Commerce — SA Competitive Landscape

**Key Competitors:**
- **iBeef (Beefmaster):** Premium beef online, broad cuts, delivery-focused
- **Baraka Beef:** "Luxury meat at supermarket prices" — price-for-value positioning, next-day Gauteng
- **The Meat Smith:** Artisan butchery, exclusive cuts, build-your-own boxes + gifting
- **Euro Meats:** Luxury processed meats, mainly B2B hospitality
- **Hokaai, Prime Cuts, Eland Butchery:** Regional premium, door-to-door, heritage branding

**Pricing Benchmarks:**
- Wagyu MS5+ strips: R450–R900+/kg retail
- Premium beef cuts: R180–R400/kg (grade-dependent)
- Most competitors use "premium/A-grade" framing — NOT discount positioning

**Key Insight:** differentiation is NOT about lowest price — it's **convenience + cold-chain reliability + curated cuts**
- Baraka's "luxury at supermarket prices" message is the most effective market positioning in SA right now
- Build-your-own box = high perceived value + easy gift format → Studex subscription should mirror this
- Regional focus first (JHB/CPT) beats national ambition before logistics are solid
- Cold-chain is a trust issue, not just logistics — market it actively

### African Coffee Markets — UAE, China, Europe 2026

**UAE:** Regional distributors + hospitality channel. Growing HORECA demand for single-origin African profiles.

**China — BREAKING (July 2026):**
- China opened market access to coffee from ALL 53 African countries — zero-tariff bilateral protocol
- Luckin Coffee sources from Brazil, Colombia, Ethiopia — **Rwanda NOT in current mix = white space**
- Luckin has 240,000-ton Brazil deal through 2029 — NOT looking for commodity competition
- **Key insight:** Rwanda is a gap in Luckin's origin portfolio — first mover advantage is real

**Europe:** Specialty roasters require Rainforest Alliance, Fair Trade, Organic certs as table stakes. Premium channel accessible with verified origin + story.

**2026 Coffee Prices:**
- ICO Composite: ~256–309 US cents/lb
- ICE Arabica: ~$2.85–$3.09/lb
- Rwandan FOB Kigali: USD 4.80/kg (~USD 2.18/lb) — competitive at specialty tier
