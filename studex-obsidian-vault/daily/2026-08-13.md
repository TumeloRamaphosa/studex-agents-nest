## 📊 Research Findings — Tonight (20:00 UTC)

### Topic 1: China-Africa Coffee — The Window Is NOW

**Key numbers:**
- China coffee market: $51.9B (2025) → $94.9B by 2031 (10.6% CAGR)
- Chinese consumption growing at **30% annually**
- Coffee from all 53 African countries: **approved July 20, 2026** (single "Green Channel" framework)
- African coffee exports to China surged **145.7% YoY** in first 5 months of 2026

**Critical margin insight:** Green bean export = $3.80/kg. Roasted coffee export = $8.50/kg. **124% margin uplift** for the same raw material.

**Studex positioning:** We are a trade bridge, not a coffee exporter.
- Scenario A: Broker Rwandan women coop (approved export) → Chinese buyer → margin on spread
- Scenario B: Become Chinese trading company's African sourcing agent → commission per container
- Scenario C: SA-China corridor — SA-processed (roasted) Rwandan-origin coffee — investigate zero-tariff eligibility

**Key differentiator:** No one is pitching "Rwanda women farmers → China specialty consumer" as a story simultaneously as a commercial opportunity. That's a PR + trade opportunity.

**⚠️ Red flags:** Rwanda has modest export volume vs Ethiopia/Kenya. Chinese buyers don't know Rwandan coffee yet. Processing/Halal certification infrastructure takes time — but Q3 2026 is still early.
