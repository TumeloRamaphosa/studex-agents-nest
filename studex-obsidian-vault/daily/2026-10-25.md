### Topic 2 — Caviar into South Africa: the permit stack nobody has assembled

- **CITES is not a nicety.** Every tin in international trade carries a CITES label and travels on a **CITES export permit**; CITES maintains a **Register of Caviar Exporters**. **Any caviar without CITES documentation is a seizure.** The supplier's permit and the registered code on each tin must be verified **before any container moves**.
- **🔴 Russia is now a legal risk, not a pricing question.** EU banned Russian caviar imports **since April 2022** and is moving on broader Russian seafood (Jun 2026). The US enacted **tariff authority up to 100% on goods imported from countries among the top importers of Russian goods** (law signed Sep 2026). **Oscietra / Sevruga are species descriptors, not origin claims** — they are fine; **"Russian" as a source claim is now a liability.** The defensible position: **farmed Chinese sturgeon with CITES documentation and full traceability** (96% of global caviar consumption is farm-raised; China is the largest farmed producer).
- **The SA permit stack — four authorities, never assembled:** **DFFE** (import permit for fish products; note the published condition that the permit **excludes freshwater fish** — ours is marine sturgeon, in scope, but check our specific permit conditions) · **ITAC** (import permit notification for frozen fish products) · **DAFF** (food import regs, importer registration) · **SARS** (importer registration + code; **Schedule 1 Part 1 Customs & Excise Tariff, 16 Oct 2026** governs the duty line) · plus **NRCS** labelling compliance.
- **⚠️ The duty rate on HS 1604.14.10 is UNVERIFIED.** Public aggregators show a 15% VAT reference; our workbook **models 20% duty**. **One lookup against the 16 Oct 2026 SARS schedule closes it.** Do not assume 20%.
- **Demand & pricing:** SA has a tracked caviar (sturgeon) import market with a published import-price series — **a comparable-market benchmark we are not using.** Global caviar **$449–519M (2024–26)**, CAGR ~7.7–8.0%, ~$812–962M by 2032–34; ME&A **$45.1M (2025) → $89.1M (2034)**. Retail 2026: Oscietra from **€1,200/kg**, Sevruga from **€800/kg**; US **$2–15/g**. **Our prices convert well:** Oscietra Imperial **R34,000/kg ≈ $2,051/kg**, Kaluga Prestige **R22,000 ≈ $1,327/kg**, Sevtruga Medium **R12,500 ≈ $754/kg** — **all three at or above the European retail reference.** The 23 Oct "below cost" alarm remains an artefact of the stale FX, as the 24 Oct recomputation concluded.
