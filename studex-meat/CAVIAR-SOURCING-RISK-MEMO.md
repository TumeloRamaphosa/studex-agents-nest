## 1. The one-paragraph version

The entire caviar line — all three tiers — is **Russian-sourced**, and the landed-cost model runs on Moscow → SA airfreight. South Africa has **no ban on Russian caviar today** (the EU does, since its 2022 sanctions package). But the risk is compounding: the Sept 2026 "targeting Russia via third countries" framework allows tariffs of **up to 100%** against goods from countries that are top importers of Russian goods, which is a **transshipment and payment-rail** risk sitting on our only supply route. Separately, **CITES** lists all Acipenseriformes, so every export needs a permit *plus* an annual national export quota set by the source state — which means **a Russian supply interruption is not hedgeable by "shopping around"**. The single highest-value action in this segment is **one farmed second origin**. Until then, the wild-Russian premium tier should be a clearly-labelled optional SKU, not the spine.

## 4. CITES and permit constraints, per source type

| Constraint | Wild (Caspian) | Farmed (EU / ZA) |
|---|---|---|
| CITES export permit | **Required** — all Acipenseriformes are listed | **Required** for international movement; **not** required for domestic ZA movement |
| National export quota | **Annual quota set by the source state** — volume *and* price are government-set | Supplier-set; not a CITES quota |
| Supply interruption hedgeability | ❌ **Not hedgeable** — quota-bound, and we have one country | ✅ Shop-around is genuinely possible |
| South African import permit | DAFF veterinary import permit (see §4a) | DAFF veterinary import permit (see §4a) |
| SA duty | HS 1604.14.10 = **25% or 200c/kg**, plus VAT — **classification open** | Same, **classification open** |

### 4a. The regulatory clock (now published honestly on the caviar page)
- **DAFF veterinary import permit** for animal/fish products must be **applied for at least 4 weeks before shipment** and takes **5–10 working days** to issue once approved.
- Our **7–14 days** is a **commercial handling lead**, counted **from the date the permit is issued** — it was previously published as the total order-to-door time, which was a misrepresentation. Fixed 2026-10-21 (see build report).
- **First shipment ≈ two months end to end.** Repeat orders are faster because the standing permit reduces the lead.
- **Open broker question:** the exact SA HS line and duty for our **roe vs prepared-caviar presentation** — in Malaysia a High Court ruled fish roe imports exempt from caviar tariffs on classification grounds. Not an SA rule, but a signal. **Do not publish a duty number until the broker answers in writing.**
