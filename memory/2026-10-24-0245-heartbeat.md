### Lesson
**A guard that only covers what it scans is decoration.** Three checkers ran
green over a credential that was live, published, and reachable with a 200.
The blind spot was not "the regex was wrong" — it was that no rule existed for
*prefix-less credentials stored under bare names*, and the exposure checker
had no GoDaddy family to begin with. A checker with no family for a provider is
as invisible as a rule with no match.

Also: **a literal key in an untracked file is still a leak** if any automated
job runs `git add .`. Untracked is not safe.
