### ✅ The guard: `ops/memory-shrink-guard.py` (+ `ops/test-memory-shrink-guard.py`)
**Rule:** every `##`-level section present in the *committed* MEMORY.md must still be present
in the staged file, unless it is listed in `ops/memory-shrink-allowlist.txt`. Structure, not
prose — rewording, reordering, adding, and removing bullet lines inside a section are all
fine. Only a whole section vanishing is a failure.

🔑 **The obvious rule was measured and rejected.** "The last line must end in terminal
punctuation" gave **58 false positives across 408 prose .md files** in this repo
(`memory/2026-07-20.md` ends `- HEARTBEAT_OK`; `campaign-brief.md` ends
`, LinkedIn copy, ad creatives on request`). A rule that fires on correct files teaches people
to ignore it. The check is therefore scoped to MEMORY.md and keyed on structure.
🔑 **"Ends without a newline" is NOT a truncation signal here** — HEAD:MEMORY.md and AGENTS.md
also end without one. A guard built on that would have blocked correct commits.

**Wired into `.githooks/pre-commit`** (the hook is installed: `core.hooksPath = .githooks`),
so `git add .` cannot publish the loss. Registered in `ops/run-tests.sh`.

**Proven, not assumed** — `ops/test-memory-shrink-guard.py`: **14 passed, 0 failed**, including
the negative controls (a dropped section FAILS; the loss is named; an allowlisted drop passes;
