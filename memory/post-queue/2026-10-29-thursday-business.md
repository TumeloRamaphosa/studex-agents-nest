## Generated Post Content

**Why Enterprises Should Buy Lead Time, Not Just Price.**

Enterprise buyers spend weeks negotiating unit price, then discover the real constraint was never the price. It was the clock.

Take our caviar import. The DAFF veterinary import permit must be applied for at least four weeks before shipment, takes five to ten working days to issue once approved, and commercial handling adds seven to fourteen days — counted from the date the permit is issued, not the date you order. A first shipment is roughly two months end to end. A buyer planning against the purchase-order date has already missed the quarter.

Coffee is no different: five to seven weeks from Kigali to Shanghai, because the route runs through Djibouti and then onto the water.

The practical consequence is working capital. Every import commits funds long before the container lands. Enterprises that plan against actual lead time price the whole programme, not just the line item — and they can commit to a delivery date they have actually met.

Ask us for the lead time on your next line item before you commit to it.
