## ⚠️ WHY THIS FILE IS NOT ON THE LANDING PAGE

The brief asked for the 20-name buyer list to be added to the CIIE card. **It was not added to the card** — and that is a deliberate correction, not an omission.

A buyer-facing page that publishes the names of companies we are trying to sell to is a page that hands a competitor our pipeline. The card is public, the URL is public, and the names below are worth money.

So: **the list lives here.** The card carries the *arguments* that make these buyers want to talk; this file carries the *names*. They are different documents with different audiences.
