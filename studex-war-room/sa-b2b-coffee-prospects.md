## SEGMENT OVERVIEW

**SA Coffee Market: ~R12bn annually**
- HORECA (Hotels/Restaurants/Cafes): ~55% of market
- OCS (Office Coffee Service): fastest-growing segment
- Pain points driving buying decisions: quality consistency, origin story, cold brew capability, delivery reliability, barista training

**Studex's white space:**
No SA coffee player combines: premium beans + brand storytelling + barista training.
Our angle: white-label bags with QR origin stories + monthly training workshops → loyal B2B contracts.
