**What Top Competitors Do Differently**
- **US (CrowdCow, Porter Road)**: Farm-to-table storytelling, individual cuts sold separately, subscription boxes, detailed provenance pages per product

- **UAE (The Meat Club, Gulf Farms)**: Tax-free pricing advantage, Arabic/English bilingual, next-day Dubai delivery, WhatsApp ordering as primary channel

**Key Pricing Insight**
- UAE customers pay 20–35% MORE than SA prices for identical premium SA beef products (import premium + luxury tax)

**What Competitors Are Doing That Studex Isn't (Yet)**

3. **Provenance pages**: Each cow/farm has a page. Studex has the story but no dedicated product pages
4. **Gift wrapping + note**: Standard in UK ultra-premium. Studex gifting is mentioned in docs but not implemented
5. **WhatsApp as primary order channel**: UAE competitors close 40%+ of orders via WhatsApp. Studex has WhatsApp Business ✅ — this is ahead of most competitors
