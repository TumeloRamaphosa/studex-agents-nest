## Topic 1: Agent Automation Tools (Blotato, n8n, Make.com)

### Key Findings

**Blotato:**
- Social media automation platform — FB, IG, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok
- AI content generation + bulk scheduling
- ~$29/month starting price
- **Already integrated into Studex stack** (MCP tool in /workspace/blotato.ts)
- **Current status: 0 accounts connected** — needs onboarding

**n8n:**
- Open-source workflow automation, self-hostable
- 2026 upgrades: multi-agent orchestration, enhanced LLM integration, memory/persistence
- Strong for: connecting Studex tools (Shopify, AgentMail, ClickUp, Discord) into automated workflows

## Topic 2: South Africa B2B Coffee Market

### Key Findings

**Market Size:**
- R4–6 billion annually (HORECA + offices)
- Urban centers driving growth: Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban

**Pain Points (Opportunities for Studex):**
- Inconsistent supply from distributors
- Import dependency + Rand volatility = unpredictable pricing
- Limited specialty-grade beans outside major cities
- Roasting capacity concentrated in Western Cape + Gauteng
- Equipment maintenance + barista training support largely absent

**Opportunities:**
- Reliable specialty coffee supply to hotels/restaurants/cafes
- Coffee + biltong/caviar bundled offerings (luxury gifting angle)
- Training + equipment as a service (differentiation)
