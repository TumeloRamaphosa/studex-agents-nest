## Topic 1: 🌍 AFRICAN COFFEE MARKET — UAE, China, Europe

### Key Findings

**🇨🇳 CHINA — BOMBSHELL: Zero-Tariff Open Door (July 20, 2026)**
- China granted coffee bean imports from ALL 53 African countries with diplomatic ties — single continent-wide "Green Channel" framework replacing individual bilateral agreements
- **Zero tariff** on African coffee into China
- Phytosanitary requirements unified under GAC Announcement No. 68 of 2026
- Chinese imports of African coffee already hit **$246M in 2025** — and this was BEFORE the policy opened
- Ethiopian processor Awo Coffee: 90% of roasted exports now go to China; exported 140 tonnes green + 20 tonnes processed in 2024 alone, growing ~10%/year
- **Direct trade** now possible: Chinese buyers can bypass European/North American middlemen → cuts costs up to **30%**, increases African farmer profitability
- China's own specialty coffee demand is soaring; projected to become **largest destination for Ethiopian coffee within 3 years**

**🇦🇪 UAE — $4.5B Market, Doubling by 2032**
- UAE coffee market: ~$4.5B (2025), projected to reach **$4.7B by 2032** at 10.8% CAGR
- Specialty roasters (RAW Coffee Company, Ludlow Coffee Group) paying **USD 20–40/kg** for traceable African coffee
- At World of Coffee Dubai 2026: buyers want distinctive flavour, stronger brand presentation, closer producer relationships
- Key buyers: HORECA sector (hotels, restaurants, cafes), premium supermarket chains, specialty importers
- Opportunity: Uganda/Rwanda Arabica has the flavour profile this market rewards

**🇪🇺 EUROPE — EUDR Compliance Now Table Stakes**
- EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) compliance required for all coffee exports to Europe
- Fair Trade / Rainforest Alliance certifications increasingly demanded by European buyers
- Price benchmarks for green Arabica: Switzerland ($36.71/kg), Spain ($26.90/kg), Australia ($22.07/kg) — premium for quality/origin
