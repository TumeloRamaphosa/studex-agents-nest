## TOPIC 1: South Africa B2B Coffee Market

### How Hotels/Restaurants/Offices Buy Coffee

- **Procurement process:** Formal buyers use supplier tenders or foodservice distributors (Bidfood, Foodspec, Celsius). Informal/smaller operators buy direct from roasters or cash-and-carry (Makro, UFC).
- **Decision makers:** Chefs/F&B managers (hotels), owners (restaurants), office managers or facilities teams (corporate).
- **What they value:** Consistency, equipment support, price per cup, delivery reliability. Origin/sustainability story is a bonus, not a driver — unless positioning as a premium establishment.
- **Pain points (documented):**
  - Equipment breakdowns + slow service from big distributors
  - Lack of flexible/smaller-batch suppliers who can deliver on short notice
  - No single supplier covers everything (coffee + machines + maintenance)
  - Inconsistent quality between batches

### SA B2B Coffee Market Size
- Foodservice coffee market: USD $50.69B globally (2026) → $70.99B by 2034 (4.3% CAGR)
- B2B channel = ~60% of all coffee procurement globally
- SA's咖啡 market growing but fragmented — no dominant D2C-to-B2B bridge player

### Key Buyer Segments & Opportunity

| Segment | Buyer | Decision Driver | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Premium hotels** | F&B Manager | Provenance story, guest experience, exclusivity | HIGH — willing to pay premium for differentiation |
| **Boutique restaurants** | Owner/Chef | Quality, consistency, brand fit | MEDIUM — smaller orders, relationship-driven |
| **Corporate offices** | Facilities | Cost per cup, simplicity, equipment reliability | LOW-MEDIUM — commoditised, price-sensitive |
| **Fast-casual/cafés** | Owner | Taste, freshness, delivery speed | MEDIUM — recurring, loyal if quality holds |

### Pricing Benchmarks (B2B)
- Wholesale green coffee: R80–R180/kg (specialty SA)
- Roasted B2B: R250–R450/kg range
- Per-cup cost at R25–R35/cup (hotels/restaurants), markup to R45–R80/cup to customer
- Office coffee: R350–R600/kg supplied, R8–R15/cup

### Who Is Buying African Coffee in UAE/China/Europe?
- **UAE:** Dubai HoReCa sector actively sourcing African origins (Ethiopian Yirgacheffe, Kenyan AA) for specialty cafes. Dubai International Airport + luxury hotel expansion driving demand.
- **China:** Growing middle class driving premium coffee culture. African single-origin in demand in tier-1 cities (Shanghai, Beijing). Tariff barriers on roasted coffee (15–20%). Green bean imports easier.
- **Europe:** Established demand for African origins. Ethiopian, Kenyan, Rwandan, Tanzanian. Sustainability certification (Rainforest Alliance) increasingly required by EU buyers.
