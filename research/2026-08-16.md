## TOPIC 2: SA B2B Coffee — Commercial Intelligence

### Key Findings

**Market context:**
- SA HORECA coffee channel growing at 4.8% CAGR through 2033
- SA foodservice market: USD 10.16B (2025) → USD 20.11B (2030) at 14.63% CAGR — fastest-growing category is premium/specialty
- Specialty coffee expanding beyond JHB/CPT into secondary cities
- Out-of-home RTD coffee in SA: 4% CAGR through 2033

**How SA café/hotel buyers actually choose suppliers:**
1. **Taste first** — cupping session is non-negotiable before any contract
2. **Consistency** — if the blend changes between shipments, they walk
3. **Story they can sell** — origin, farm, cooperative; must be verifiable (QR code, documentation)
4. **Delivery reliability** — most pain point in SA; cold brew capability adds urgency (suppliers caught flat-footed)
5. **Price stability** — 12-month contracts with no surprise increases wins loyalty
6. **Training support** — barista upskilling included with supply = retainer, not one-off sale

**Critical gap in current outreach plan:**
- No mention of **cupping session offer** in any template (this is the #1 foot-in-the-door)
- Cold brew mention is there but **no lead time or pricing** for cold brew specific SKU
- September 1 launch is 2 weeks away — no evidence of Rwanda A1/A2 sample shipment confirmation

**OCS (Office Coffee Service) — SA white space:**
- OCS market: USD 9.8B (2025) → USD 20.59B (2034) at 8.6% CAGR
- No SA OCS player offers barista training. Studex's training hook converts bean supply into **retainer contracts**
