## TOPIC 1: African Coffee — UAE, China & Europe Buyer Landscape

### Key Findings

- **🇨🇳 China opened African coffee imports July 20, 2026** — 53 countries under single continent-wide framework. This is the biggest policy shift in African coffee history. Zero tariffs, formalised channel. The window is NOW.
- **China's coffee market: $42B (2024), up 150% since 2010.** 21% annual consumption growth. 240,000+ metric tons/year. Starbucks, Luckin, Manner are all scaling. They need supplier relationships NOW before others lock in.
- **Europe demands traceability as baseline.** Buyers want proof of farm-of-origin, processing method, and certification (PROWTC, Fair Trade, Rainforest Alliance). Commodity coffee stagnating; specialty certified is growing. SA green coffee at $16.42/kg vs Belgium $13.25/kg — SA is already premium-positioned.
- **UAE is the GCC hub** — Dubai imports re-export to Saudi, Qatar, Kuwait. UAE food service sector growing. Premium African coffee gaining shelf space in Dubai duty-free and specialty retailers.
- **CIIE 2026 (Nov 5-10, Shanghai) already sold 18,000 boxes of coffee in 6 hours in 2022.** Buyers are pre-registered. Rwanda PROWTC A1/A2 product + Studex as exporter = strong story. Booth + buyer meeting schedule needed by September.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **China first-mover advantage** — PROWTC Rwanda coffee via Studex to Chinese specialty roasters. Relationship-building now = supply contracts by Q1 2027.
2. **UAE gateway strategy** — Partner with a Dubai-based importer/distributor. Let them handle GCC re-export while Studex focuses on production.
3. **Europe: traceable is premium** — Lead with PROWTC certification story. SA at $16.42/kg vs commodity at $13/kg — the $3/kg premium covers certification + logistics.

## TOPIC 2: Luxury Meat E-Commerce — SA Competitive Landscape

### Key Findings

- **SA Biltong (sabiltong.co.za):** Whole beef biltong 1kg = R499. Premium positioning, pure e-commerce play. Direct-to-consumer nationwide delivery.
- **Best Biltong (bestbiltong.com):** Targets expat South Africans globally. International shipping a core capability. Key differentiator: "taste of home" for diaspora.
- **Venisons Co (venisons.co.za):** Game meat specialist — kudu, springbok, eland biltong. Niche positioning at R350-500/kg range. No明显的 luxury brand story — product-first.
- **Food Lover's Market:** Mass market biltong at retail. R200-350/kg range. Competing on price, not story.
- **E-commerce SA market: $38B (2025) → $77B by 2034** — 8.17% CAGR. Amazon SA launched. Global players forcing local retailers to compete on CX + speed.
- **Biltong expat market is underserved globally.** "The Biltong Master" selling into Asia via Facebook/YouTube with viral videos. No premium SA brand owns the global biltong e-commerce market.
