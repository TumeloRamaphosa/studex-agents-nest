## TOPIC 1 — COFFEE: THE MARKET TURNED AGAINST US WHILE WE WERE SLEEPING

### Key findings

- **Correction to last night's brief (2026-10-22).** We wrote *"buyer leverage is high this quarter, prices favour importers."* **That is now wrong.** ICE Arabica C closed **$3.06/lb on 23 Oct** — **+11.66% over the past month**, highest since August. Still −27% YoY, but the "cheap green" window has narrowed sharply. Any CIIE quote built on the 21 Oct price sheet is priced against a market that has moved against it.
- **Our China FOB price sheet is priced BELOW the commodity benchmark.** Sheet says **USD 4.80/kg FOB Kigali** for Rwanda A1 washed. ICE C at $3.06/lb = **$6.75/kg** green-equivalent. **We are quoting ~29% under the benchmark** (4.80 vs 6.75). Either we have a genuine deep specialty discount (great — that is our margin) or the number is stale from a softer 2025 market. **This is the single most valuable thing to check before CIIE.** (Caveat: ICE C is a composite for exchange-grade milds; a wide differential to a premium A1 lot is normal. A −29% differential is not.)
- **Rwanda just set a world-beating specialty record — on 21 October, three days ago.** Best of Rwanda 2026 online auction: top lot (Geisha, K-Organics, Huye) at **US$452/kg ≈ RWF670,000**, **~66× the average international market price**. 31 lots selected from 320 samples; 20 winners scoring **87.49%–91.81%**. The 2026 auction sample set sells at **US$250 per 100g**. ⚠️ Note the headline is a *micro-lot Geisha* — do not quote $452/kg as "our price" or as the Rwandan market price. **Do** use it as proof of the quality ceiling we are exporting from.
- **Farm-gate is inflating:** NAEB set Rwanda's 2026 minimum cherry price at **RWF750/kg, +25%** on 2025 (RWF600). Export-side cost pressure is real for Q1 2027 contracts.
- **FX is the lever nobody is watching.** USD/ZAR closed **16.56 on 23 Oct** (ZARONIA 16.4688; rand **weakened** over the past month). Our cost workbook hardcodes **18.5**. At 16.56 every USD-denominated landed cost in the business is **~10.5% cheaper in rand than our models say** — caviar, coffee, freight, the lot.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **Re-price, don't re-bargain.** With USD 4.80/kg FOB under a $6.75/kg benchmark, the correct CIIE ask is a band, not a legacy number — and the honest band is anchored to C minus a modest differential for specialty A1, with a floor we can still hit if the auction-grade differential is wide.
2. **Best of Rwanda is a 12-day marketing asset that costs nothing.** CIIE is 12 days out and the most newsworthy coffee fact in the world is 11 days old and ours to tell. It positions us as an exporter of the *top of the ladder*, which is the only position that survives a rising green market.
3. **Rustica-level buyers are now our customer.** At $3.06/lb, commercial-grade buyers can't tell origins apart on price — they separate on cup, traceability and story. That is precisely our lane (Rwanda A1/A2, Rainforest Alliance/PROWTC per the sheet — both claims still unverified per the registry).
4. **FX tailwind is unbanked.** Every USD-priced quote we send is currently 10.5% more competitive in rand terms than our own model assumed. Use the margin: protect it as margin, don't leak it into price.

## TOPIC 2 — AGENT AUTOMATION: A FREE VOICE ENGINE, AND THREE DEADLINES

### Key findings

- **Voicebox (github.com/jamiepine/voicebox) is a MIT-licensed, local-first AI voice studio** — and it is the most underrated asset on tonight's list. Voice cloning from **3 seconds** of audio; **7 TTS engines** (Qwen3-TTS 1.7B/0.6B, Chatterbox, Chatterbox Turbo, LuxTTS, Qwen CustomVoice, TADA, Kokoro 82M); **Whisper dictation in 99 languages**; a built-in **MCP server** (`http://127.0.0.1:17493/mcp`, tool `voicebox.speak`) **and** a plain REST `POST /speak` for non-MCP callers; Linux binaries and source build; personality/rewrite layer (a persona can rewrite copy in character).
  - **Studex fit — real:** (a) *Kokoro* is 82M, CPU, real-time → **runs on our 2-CPU / 8GB Orgo VM with no GPU**; *LuxTTS* is ~1GB VRAM, **150× realtime on CPU** → same. (b) **Zero per-minute cost** against a paid ElevenLabs key we are already paying for. (c) **Cloned brand voice** — a 3-second clip of Tumelo becomes the voice of the coffee/biltong WhatsApp line, and it never leaves our infrastructure.
  - **The honest constraint:** Voicebox is a *speech-out* engine. It does not answer the phone. To run customer service you still need (i) inbound voice capture — WhatsApp Business API voice notes or a telephony leg, (ii) ASR on the inbound side, (iii) the agent brain, (iv) a policy/script layer, (v) a human handoff. **That is a multi-day build, not a Build Hour build.** Tonight's job was to establish that the *voice output tier is free and self-hosted* — which materially de-risks a customer-service roadmap, and nothing more.
- **Reddit's public Data API closes.** New public-API requests stop **31 Oct 2026**; RSS **13 Nov**; unregistered apps cut off **12 Jan 2027**; all public access ends **March 2027**. We have no Reddit dependency I can see — but any sentiment/scraping assumption in the analyst stack dies on 31 Oct. Flotato/Meta-side sentiment must not be built on Reddit.
- **X is now pay-per-use and expensive for us:** **$0.015/post, but $0.20/post if the post contains a link.** Our entire social model is link-in-bio posts. **Meta now mandates AI content labelling** (`is_ai_generated`, in force since 22 Jun 2026) — our AI-generated biltong/coffee creative must be labelled, or we risk distribution.
- **n8n shipped 2.43 on 6 Oct: Browser Use is now available to all users**, plus an instance-level MCP server and workflow settings editable over MCP. That is a browser agent inside our workflow engine — the "browse, price-check a competitor, draft the reply" loop is now buildable without a paid browser service. **Make's Maia** (13 Aug) does the same thing in plain language but is a paid SaaS with per-operation credits; we self-host n8n.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **A self-hosted voice tier for customer service, at zero marginal cost.** Not now — but the economics are now known: we can serve voice replies on our own VM without a per-minute vendor. That changes the shape of any WhatsApp customer-service plan (Kigali/CIIE lead follow-up is exactly where a branded voice reply earns its keep).
2. **n8n Browser Use + MCP server = a competitor-price watcher that costs no browser API.** Our price-vs-Checkers/Woolworths/biltong-comps problem (already flagged in the 23 Oct brief) can be automated on our own stack.
3. **One-hour protection against three deadline risks:** Reddit (stop building on it), X link-post cost (account for $0.20/post in the content plan), Meta AI labelling (audit our generated creative before the next post).

### Recommended actions

- [ ] **Rule Reddit out of the sentiment stack** — one line in the agents' research config. 31 Oct.

## TERTIARY — CAVIAR PRICING FLOOR: RECOMPUTED, AND THE ALARM WAS AN ARTEFACT

Last night (23 Oct brief) I reported we were selling trade caviar **below our own modelled floor**. **That finding does not survive live FX.** Recomputed tonight at **USD/ZAR 16.56** (not the 18.5 hardcoded in `caviar-landed-cost.xlsx`), duty 20% as modelled, 30% margin floor:

| SKU | Landed @16.56 (VAT in cost) | 30% floor | Published trade | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oscietra Imperial | R23,624 | R33,750 | R34,000 | ✅ **above floor** |
| Kaluga Prestige | R16,768 | R23,950 | R22,000 | ⚠️ 8% under |
| Sevtruga Medium | R9,913 | R14,160 | R12,500 | ⚠️ 12% under |

**And if import VAT is recoverable** (which a VAT-registered importer claims back — it should not sit in a price floor at all): Oscietra floor **R29,460** ✅, Kaluga **R20,940** ✅, Sevtruga **R12,420** vs published R12,500 ✅ — **all three clear.**

**Read:** at the real exchange rate, and on the correct VAT treatment, **our published trade prices are above the floor on every SKU.** The "we are selling below cost" alarm was caused by a stale 18.5 FX rate plus recoverable tax treated as cost. **Do not reprice the caviar line on last night's finding.** The clearing-agent email (`outbound-drafts/clearing-agent-caviar-duty-rebate.md`) is still the right next move — but it is now a **confirmation, not a rescue**, and it stops being urgent.

**Two housekeeping actions follow:**
