| Competitor | Product | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SA Biltong** | A-Grade Whole Beef Biltong 1kg | **R499** | Budget/accessible tier |
| **SA Biltong** | Mixed cuts | from R319 | Entry pricing |
| **Baraka Beef** | Beef biltong | ~R450–550/kg | "Luxury at supermarket prices" — undercutting |
| **iBeef** | Premium biltong | ~R500–600/kg | SA premium positioning |
| **The Meat Smith** | Craft biltong + wagyu | R800+/kg | Ultra-premium tier |
| **Euro Meats** | Imported luxury proteins | Market rate | Hospitality supply focus |

**Critical finding:** SA Biltong and Baraka Beef are both fighting at R450–550/kg. This compresses the mid-market. **Studex must NOT position as cheapest — not viable at quality prices.**

**Pricing gap identified:**
- Budget biltong: R300–400/kg → crowded, thin margins
- Ultra-premium craft: R800+/kg → The Meat Smith owns this
- **White space: R450–650/kg "accessible luxury"** — emerald branding + story + cold-chain trust messaging
