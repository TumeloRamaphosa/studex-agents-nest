## TOPIC 2: South Africa B2B Coffee — Hotel, Restaurant & Office Buyers

### Key Findings

- **SA coffee market**: ~R12 billion annually (retail + food service). Three segments: retail ( supermarkets), food service (hotels/restaurants/cafés), and office coffee services. Food service is the entry point for B2B premium coffee.
- **Who buys for hotels/restaurants**: Executive chefs and F&B managers source coffee through: (1) specialist coffee importers (Toddy Café, Supply Chain, Coffee Tree), (2) broadline distributors (Makro, Premier), (3) direct from roasters. Decision cycle: 3-6 months. Relationships matter. References from other hotels are the #1 trust signal.
- **Pain points**: (1) Inconsistent quality between batches — chefs hate this, (2) Late deliveries — restaurants run out mid-service, (3) Lack of origin story — hotel marketers want a "coffee provenance" angle for their menus, (4) Barista training support — most hotels have undertrained bar staff.
- **The Rwanda angle**: No SA hotel chain currently markets Rwanda as an origin. Ethiopian and Colombian dominate. PROWTC certification (which Studex has) is a differentiator for hotel sustainability stories. This is a white space.
- **Office coffee market**: Contracted service model — companies pay per month per employee for bean/equipment rental. Market leaders in SA: Starbucks Office, Coffee Culture, Amigo Coffee. These are service businesses, not just bean sellers. Entry point: offer a "first month free" pilot to corporate offices.

### Opportunities for Studex (B2B Coffee SA)

1. **SA hotel lobby first**: Target 4-star hotels in Sandton, Cape Town Foreshore, and Kruger area. Pitch: "Africa's finest — Rwanda single-origin, PROWTC certified, direct trade." Include a sample 1kg bag. Turn the hotel coffee into a story (menu card with farm details).
2. **Restaurant wine-paired coffee**: Fine dining restaurants pair coffee like wine. Approach sommeliers and heads of F&B directly — not procurement. Sell the Rwanda flavor profile (jasmine, stone fruit) as a menu differentiator.
3. **Corporate office pilot**: Offer a 1-month free trial to 3-5 Sandton offices. Include the full setup (borrowed grinder/express machine if needed). Lock in 12-month supply contract after trial.
4. **The wedding season play**: SA wedding season = May–October. Hotels and wedding venues need coffee for espresso bars. Pre-position inventory for bulk orders.
