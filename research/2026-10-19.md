**Market Size & Growth**
- Global coffee market: $256B in 2026 → $397B by 2035 (CAGR 5.2%)
- SA specialty coffee market: growing, more cafés opening in JHB/CPT (Perfect Daily Grind, Mar 2026)
- Foodservice coffee market: $50.7B in 2026 → $71B by 2034
- **B2B segment = 59.8% of market** (cafés, restaurants, offices, institutional buyers)
- Coffee subscription market: $1.02B → $2.95B by 2035 (CAGR 12.1%) — fast-growing

**Who the Buyers Are (from sa-b2b-coffee-prospects.md)**
- **Top hotels (JHB/CPT):** Saxon, Four Seasons Westcliff, Table Bay, Cape Grace, One&Only, Ellerman House
