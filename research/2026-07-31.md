## TOPIC 1: Agent Automation Stack — What Studex Has & What's Next

### Key Findings

- **Blotato is semi-active but blind.** API confirmed working. 3 accounts connected (FB, YouTube, IG). Problem: Meta token expired Jun 22 — Blotato is posting into the void. No visibility on whether content actually published. Re-authentication is the single unlock that makes the whole content pipeline alive again.

- **Voicebox is a local AI voice studio** (open-source, github.com/jamiepine/voicebox). Key capabilities: voice cloning from seconds of audio, 7 TTS engines, dictation into any app, 23 languages, runs 100% locally (privacy-first). Can give any MCP-aware AI agent a voice. Free alternative to ElevenLabs + WisprFlow combined.

- **Voicebox = WhatsApp voice layer for Studex.** Studex already has the ElevenLabs API key. Voicebox is complementary — it adds INPUT (dictation, voice calls) where ElevenLabs handles OUTPUT (speech generation). For a meat business on WhatsApp, voice ordering and voice customer service are massive.

- **n8n is the missing workflow glue.** Open-source, self-hostable, 400+ app integrations. Studex's current problem: Charlie, Naledi, and Robusca operate as siloed agents. n8n can orchestrate them — trigger Charlie when a Shopify order arrives, push approved content to Blotato automatically, route WhatsApp messages to the right agent.

- **Current Studex automation is agent-first, workflow-second.** The agents (Charlie, Naledi, Robusca) are sophisticated. The plumbing between them (webhooks, triggers, approvals) is manual. n8n fills that gap without code.

## TOPIC 2: Luxury Meat E-Commerce South Africa

### Key Findings

- **SA biltong market is fragmented, not dominated by big D2C brands.** Most premium biltong is sold through delis, farm stalls, and word of mouth. The digital shelf is wide open for a brand that can combine great product with great storytelling and reliable delivery.

- **The "Amazon of meat" gap is a real opportunity.** Temperature-controlled delivery is the core unsolved problem. Most SA e-commerce players either avoid fresh meat entirely or charge a fortune for it. Solving cold-chain logistics at reasonable cost = competitive moat.

- **Woolworths, Checkers, and Spar are all increasing their premium meat D2C push.** They have logistics but lack authenticity and storytelling. A niche like "wildlife ranch biltong" or "heritage breed beef" beats them on brand, not price.

- **WhatsApp ordering is the highest-converting meat channel in SA right now.** Customers text an order, pay via EFT, and expect delivery. This is manual work. Automating this with an agent (Charlie or Robusca) + WhatsApp Business API = scalable without per-order human time.

- **Content is the growth lever.** Naledi (CMO) has been silent 11 days. That pipeline is stalled. Every day without fresh content = lower algorithm visibility = fewer discovery customers. The content engine is Studex's unfair advantage — when it runs, it works.
