## TOPIC 1: China Opens to ALL African Coffee — July 20, 2026 is a Historic Date

## TOPIC 2: Voicebox — What It Is, Can It Power Studex Customer Service?

### Key Findings

- **Voicebox (github.com/jamiepine/voicebox) is an open-source local-first AI voice studio.** MIT license. Key capabilities: voice cloning from 3 seconds of audio, speech generation in 23 languages via 7 engines (Qwen3-TTS, Kokoro, etc.), and speech-to-text dictation.
- **v0.5.0 released recently** — expanded multi-engine capabilities. Actively developed.
- **What it is NOT:** It is not a server-deployable production voice agent platform. It is a desktop creative tool — voice cloning, audio production, TTS experiments. Think Audacity meets ElevenLabs.
- **Production customer service voice:** ElevenLabs is the standard for production deployments (full API, web-accessible, enterprise-ready). Voicebox is for prototyping and local experimentation.
- **The gap:** A proper Studex voice customer service agent would need: ElevenLabs (TTS) + a dialogue engine (n8n/Claude) + WhatsApp/phone channel. Voicebox is not the answer here.

### Opportunities for Studex (Voice)
