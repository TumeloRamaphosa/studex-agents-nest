## Topic 1: South Africa HORECA Meat Buyers (Hotels, Restaurants, Catering)

### Key Findings

- **SA HORECA market is underserved for premium local meat.** Most high-end hotels and restaurants import protein (Wagyu from Japan/Australia, premium beef from EU) because local supply chains are fragmented and inconsistent. This is Studex's opening.
- **Procurement pain points are real:** Suppliers don't deliver reliably, quality varies batch-to-batch, and buyers can't get traceability docs (HACCP, import permits). A premium local supplier with consistent quality + documentation wins long-term contracts.
- **Direct farm sourcing is trending globally** — SA hotels are starting to bypass wholesalers and go direct to producers. Studex should position as "farm-to-table B2B" with the right certifications.
- **Catering and events is the fastest HORECA segment** — year-end functions, corporate events, weddings Dec–Jan are massive. Premium biltong platters as cocktail fare is an untapped category — nobody is selling biltong as an events product.
- **Restaurants want storytelling** — provenance, breed, story behind the meat drives menu pricing power. "Wagyu biltong from grass-fed cattle" on a menu justifies R800+/kg pricing.

### Opportunities for Studex
1. **B2B meat catalogue** — WhatsApp/AgentMail-order catalogue for SA restaurants: curated cuts + biltong + wagyu biltong. One message to order, one invoice, next-day delivery.
2. **Events catering platters** — Package biltong boards as corporate event cocktail fare. Target: Cape Town + Joburg corporate event planners.
3. **Hotel supply contracts** — 3–5 star hotels that currently import Wagyu. Offer SA Wagyu biltong + premium cuts at lower landed cost than imports, with full traceability docs.

### Recommended Actions
- Draft a HORECA sell sheet (1-pager PDF) with product catalogue, pricing, delivery terms, and certifications. Distribute via WhatsApp to restaurant contacts.
- Add "Events & Catering" category to studex-meat.com — order biltong platters for year-end functions.
