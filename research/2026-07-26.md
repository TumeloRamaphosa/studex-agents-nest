## TOPIC 1: South Africa B2B Coffee — Horeca & Office Buyers

### Key Findings

- **SA Out-of-Home Coffee Market:** Worth USD 43.31B in 2026, growing at 4.11% CAGR to USD 52.98B by 2031. Specialty coffee is the growth driver — commodity coffee is stagnating.
- **SA Specialty Coffee Surge:** More specialty cafés opening in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, Sandton. Events culture (coffee cuppings, brew competitions) driving demand for traceable, single-origin beans. Source: Perfect Daily Grind (March 2026).
- **Horeca Buyer Profile (SA):**
  - **Hotels:** Buy via distributors (Deluxe Coffee, Coffee Tech, Cape Town Coffee Roasters). Want: consistency, traceability, breakfast/kitchen-grade pricing. Pain point: inconsistent single-origin quality, USD price volatility.
  - **Restaurants/Gastro pubs:** Smaller orders, want "locally sourced" story for menu differentiation. Pain point: no direct farm-to-table supplier exists at SME scale.
  - **Offices (B2B supply):** Rent-to-roast or capsule service. Players: vida e caffè, Coffeeworks, local operators. Pain point: bland commodity coffee, no premium offering.
- **Buying behavior:** Horeca buyers source via distributors, rarely direct from farms. Switching cost is LOW if you offer: (1) cupping samples, (2) net-30 terms, (3) consistent quality. The distributor margin (30-40%) is the opportunity — bypass them with direct farm-to-Horeca pricing.
- **CIIE Nov 2026 is the target:** China market window opened July 20. PROWTC Rwanda A1/A2 is the product. But SA Horeca is a near-term revenue opportunity before export kicks in.

### Opportunities for Studex
1. **Farm-to-Horeca positioning** — Be the first SA supplier offering fully traceable Rwandan/SA green coffee with PROWTC certification to boutique hotels and gastro restaurants. Distributor bypass = competitive pricing.
2. **Sample-led sales** — Offer free 500g cupping samples to SA Horeca buyers. Low cost, high conversion.
3. **Office coffee service** — Partner with a local roaster to offer a "premium office coffee" subscription. Studex supplies green, roaster handles roasting + delivery.
