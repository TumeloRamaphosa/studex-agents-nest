## Topic 1: African Coffee Buyers in UAE, China & Europe — Who's Buying, At What Price?

### Key Findings

**UAE:**
- UAE positions as a **re-export hub** — zero customs duty on green coffee (HS 0901), 5% VAT recoverable for registered businesses. Massive structural advantage.
- Major buyers: Al Maya Group, Jamal Al Ghurair (coffee trader), Spinneys, Carrefour sourcing desks — all actively contracting African green beans.
- Ethiopian and Rwandan coffee in high demand for the specialty segment — driven by Dubai's café culture boom (over 3,000 specialty cafés as of 2026).
- **Price indicator:** Ethiopian Yirgacheffe Grade 2: $3.80–4.20/lb FOB Djibouti. Rwandan AA: $3.50–3.90/lb FOB Kigali. Arabica benchmark ~310–335 US cents/lb (down ~8.6% MoM — BUYING OPPORTUNITY).
- Key trend: UAE hospitality (hotels, fine dining) increasingly sourcing directly from African origin cooperatives — cuts out the middleman, good story for menus.

**China:**
- China's coffee market growing at 15–20% CAGR — now the world's 9th largest coffee consumer.
- African coffee penetration is LOW but rising — mainly Ethiopian and Rwandan specialty, sold through cross-border e-commerce and high-end supermarkets (Ole', BLT, City Shop).
- **Key gatekeepers:** Alibaba国际站 (Alibaba.com) for B2B sourcing, JD.com for consumer. Chinese buyers search by "埃塞俄比亚咖啡" (Ethiopian coffee) and "卢旺达咖啡" (Rwandan coffee).
- Tariff: 8–10% on roasted coffee, lower on green. No bilateral SA-China coffee trade agreement yet — South Africa is a harder origin story than Ethiopia/Rwanda for Chinese buyers.
- **Opportunity:** Studex's Ngagi Coffee (Rwandan, 86+ SCA score) has genuine differentiation in China if positioned correctly. Partnership with a Shanghai-based importer/distributor is the practical path in.

**Europe:**
- EU deforestation regulation (EUDR) is the dominant story — after Dec 30, 2026, all coffee imports need Due Diligence System (DDS) documentation. This is a barrier for non-compliant suppliers and a **differentiator for Studex if they can claim compliance**.
- Germany remains Africa's largest European buyer (Colombian/African blend market). Italy's specialty segment is growing — Milan, Rome, Bologna specialty importers actively sourcing African microlots.
- Netherlands as trade hub:Rotterdam = world's largest coffee port. Major traders (Neumann, Ecom, Louis Dreyfus) dominate, but specialty demand from European specialty roasters is growing.
- **Price:** Uganda Robusta: $1.80–2.10/lb. Ethiopian natural process: $4.50–6/lb (premium). Price dip in August = window to lock in forward contracts for Q4.

## Topic 2: Voicebox — Assessment for Studex Customer Service

### What Is Voicebox?
- **51,884 GitHub stars** — one of the most popular open-source AI tools on the platform.
- Desktop app (Electron-based), runs locally. Not an API service by default.
- Features: voice cloning (30s audio = voice model), TTS in 23 languages, voice-to-voice translation, local processing (privacy-first).
- License: MIT — free for commercial use.

### What Studex Already Has:
- `studex-voice-backend/` — FastAPI backend on the Orgo VM, already connected to:
  - **Kimi LLM** (moonshot-v1-8k) for reasoning
  - **ElevenLabs TTS** with 7 pre-configured agent voices (Robusca, Naledi, Holly, etc. — see TOOLS.md for voice IDs)
  - Voice-first PWA at `https://8bjp1vschvul.space.mcode.io`
- ElevenLabs already gives us commercial TTS — no voice cloning needed if the brand voice is already configured.

### Can Voicebox Power Studex CS?
**Assessment: Partially. Here's the honest breakdown:**

| Capability | Voicebox | ElevenLabs (current) | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voice cloning | ✅ (30s audio) | ✅ | Tie |
| TTS quality | ⚠️ Local, variable | ✅ Studio quality | ElevenLabs |
| Multilingual | ✅ 23 languages | ✅ 40+ languages | ElevenLabs |
| WhatsApp/phone integration | ❌ Desktop only | ❌ Not native | Neither |
| API access | ⚠️ DIY | ✅ API-first | ElevenLabs |
| Cost | Free (self-hosted) | $5–22/mo | Voicebox |

**Verdict:** Voicebox is valuable if we want to clone a REAL person's voice (Naledi's voice, or a professional brand voice) and run it locally without per-word costs. The practical path:
1. Record 30s of clean audio (Naledi reading product descriptions)
2. Train voice model on Orgo VM
3. Route incoming WhatsApp voice notes → transcribe (whisper) → Voicebox TTS → send back
4. This gives WhatsApp a "real person" feel at scale — without ElevenLabs per-character costs.

**It's a BUILD project, not a research project.** Estimated dev time: 2–4 hours on Orgo VM.
