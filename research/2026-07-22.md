## 🏨 TOPIC 1: SA B2B COFFEE MARKET

### Key Findings

**Market size:**
- Out-of-home coffee market: **$43.31 billion (2026)**, growing to $52.98B by 2030
- SA imports most of its coffee — vulnerable to ZAR exchange rate and global price swings
- SA coffee roasters squeezed between rising green coffee costs and price-sensitive consumers — margins under pressure

**Who the buyers are:**
- **Hotels (high-end):** 4-star and above, conference facilities. Buy through preferred suppliers or direct from roasters. Want: consistency, certification (Rainforest Alliance, PROWTC), traceability story, air-freight ready small batches. Key pain: unreliable supply, quality inconsistency.
- **Restaurants/Cafés:** Independent cafés are price-sensitive, mostly buy from SA roasters (Supply Chain Group, Tricon). Chain restaurants (Spur, RocoMamas) have central procurement — hard to break in. Key pain: freshness, equipment support.
- **Offices (B2B service):** Companies like Dunkin, Coffee Junction, Rainmaker supply offices with bean-to-cup machines. They buy in bulk, lock in annual contracts. Key pain: machine downtime, coffee quality variation.
- **Spaza shops / township economy:** Massive volume, low price point. Not Wildlife Coffee's target — commoditised.

**Pain points SA buyers care about most (2026):**
1. **Load shedding** — SA power cuts kill bean-to-cup machines; buyers want energy-efficient or backup-compatible equipment
2. **Supply reliability** — SA roasters struggle with import consistency; buyers want guaranteed stock
3. **Price volatility** — Arabica prices up; buyers want fixed-price contracts or clear pass-through clauses
4. **Sustainability certification** — Hotels increasingly require Rainforest Alliance, PROWTC, Fairtrade; **Wildlife Coffee already certified on both counts**
5. **Technical support** — Espresso machine maintenance is a big complaint; buyers value suppliers who offer service contracts

**How they source:**
- Direct from roasters (preferred for hotels wanting custom blends)
- Through distributors (Tricon, Supply Chain Group, Franco)
- Through B2B service companies (Coffee Junction, Rainmaker) for offices
- Through procurement platforms ( hospitality group purchasing organizations)

**Wildlife Coffee's angle in SA:**
- PROWTC + Rainforest Alliance certified — **box already ticked** for luxury hotels
- SA roasters are customers (they buy green beans to roast), not competitors
- Pitch: "We're your specialty green bean supplier for re-export or white-label roasting"
- Alternatively: supply Wildlife Coffee branded retail bags to hotel gift shops / conference coffee breaks

## 🎙️ TOPIC 2: VOICEBOX — REALISTIC ASSESSMENT

### What Voicebox Actually Is

`jamiepine/voicebox` — open-source, local-first voice studio:
- **Voice cloning:** 10-30 seconds of audio → clone any voice (uses Qwen3-TTS)
- **Text-to-speech:** Generate speech in 23 languages
- **Dictation:** Type into any app using voice
- **Multi-track audio:** For audio production workflows

### What It Cannot Do (Important)

- **It is NOT a phone answering agent** — it's a TTS/voice cloning studio
- It cannot answer incoming phone calls in real time (no speech recognition + voice generation loop in one tool)
- For real-time two-way voice agents, you'd need: Voicebox (TTS layer) + a speech-to-text engine (Whisper/Ocean) + a reasoning model + a telephony API (Twilio/Vonage)
- That stack is more complex — Q3/Q4 project territory, not tonight

### What It CAN Do for Studex (Near-term)

**Option A: WhatsApp/Telegram voice replies**
- Customer sends a voice message → transcribed via Whisper → AI responds → voice generated via Voicebox → sent back
- Entirely text-based messaging platforms — no phone line needed
- **Use case:** Cool, but Naledi would need to build and maintain it

**Option B: Pre-recorded voice for marketing**
- Clone Tumelo's voice → generate audio ads, voicemails, order confirmations
- One-time setup, unlimited生成
- **Use case:** "Your Wildlife Coffee order is on its way" — personalized voice messages

**Option C: Customer service FAQ audio**
- Generate audio versions of FAQ responses for website
- Improves accessibility, adds premium feel

### Better Fit: Dograh for Voice Agents

From tonight's search: **Dograh** (reddit.com/r/ollama) — free open-source voice AI agent platform, drag-and-drop workflow builder like n8n, specifically for building phone call agents. Worth a deeper look.
