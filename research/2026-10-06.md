## TOPIC 1: Luxury Meat E-Commerce — Competitor Intelligence

### Key Findings

- **Biltong is going global (Economist, Sept 23 2026).** The diaspora + fitness/wellness protein trend is real. UK, USA, Australia are the top export markets. SA producers selling 200g packs at £15–£25 online. This validates the export thesis — but the window is opening fast and competition is building.
- **Best Biltong (UK-based)** is the clearest competitor to watch. Family-run SA import business with Trustpilot reviews, established checkout flow, and "South African products" branding. Sells biltong, droewors, Wagyu. Price anchor: ~£18–£25 for 200g premium biltong. Ships from UK warehouse = fast EU delivery. **This is who Studex competes with for diaspora and expat buyers.**
- **Trustpilot presence matters.** Best Biltong has 25 reviews at 4.2 stars — not overwhelming, but real social proof. Studex has Shopify with reviews but hasn't been pushed to accumulate Google/Trustpilot reviews. This is low-hanging fruit.
- **"Lekker Meat Shop" (Gqeberha)** is a domestic competitor going heavy on social media (Facebook video reels). Large product variety, strong local brand. Not an export threat but signals the domestic market is getting more competitive.
- **Studex Wagyu Biltong at R650 (sale) vs ~R800+ regular.** Competitive with Best Biltong pricing when converted — actually cheaper in ZAR terms for the same product tier. Price is not the problem. **Distribution and brand awareness in export markets is the gap.**
- **Shopify + WhatsApp + AgentMail stack** is actually ahead of most SA competitors. Most are still on plain Instagram DMs or basic websites. The automation stack is a genuine moat — if it gets connected.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **Build Trustpilot + Google Reviews NOW.** Every completed order should trigger a review request. 50 real reviews at 4.5+ changes conversion rate dramatically. Competitors have this; Studex doesn't yet.
2. **Diaspora export packs.** Package 3-pack gift boxes (Wagyu + Traditional + Droewors) for UK/US/AU buyers. Price at £45–£65. Use Blotato to run FB/IG ads targeting SA expats in London, Birmingham, Melbourne, Sydney.
3. **Cross-sell coffee.** Biltong + African coffee bundle = natural pairing. The SA origin story works for both products.
4. **Subscriptions.** Monthly biltong packs (R350–R550/month) = recurring revenue. No competitor is doing this well yet.

## TOPIC 2: Voicebox — Could It Power Studex Customer Service?

### What Is Voicebox?

**GitHub:** github.com/jamiepine/voicebox
**What it is:** Open-source local-first AI voice studio. Free, self-hosted. Clones voices from 30-second sample. Generates speech across 7 TTS engines. Global hotkey dictation. MCP protocol support (talks to AI agents). Desktop app (Mac/Windows/Linux).

### What It's NOT

- It's NOT a ready-made customer service bot
- It's NOT a voice channel tool for call centers
- It's NOT a telephony integration (no phone calls)

### What It CAN Do (Relevant to Studex)

1. **Clone Tumelo's voice** → use as branded voice for WhatsApp audio notes. Customers hear Tumelo personally. High-touch luxury feel.
2. **Agent voice responses.** When Robusca agent replies via WhatsApp, optionally convert text → Tumelo voice note and send as audio. Unique. No competitor doing this.
3. **Content creation.** Auto-generate podcast-length audio about new products using Tumelo's cloned voice. Post to WhatsApp Status / IG Stories.
4. **Dictation.** Tumelo speaks notes → Voicebox transcribes and routes to agent for action. Useful for order updates spoken in.

### Constraints

- Self-hosted = needs the Orgo VM or a local machine running Voicebox
- WhatsApp audio messages = manual send or needs n8n integration for auto-send
- Not a turnkey solution — requires 2–4 hours to set up voice clone + integration
- **Verdict: Interesting for Q4 project. Not an tonight Build Hour item.**

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **Branded voice = competitive differentiation.** No SA biltong company has a founder voice. This is content marketing gold.
2. **WhatsApp audio catalogue.** Send customers a voice note about new products instead of text. Higher engagement.

### Recommended Actions
- [ ] DEFER to next Build Hour (after Blotato and n8n are live)
- [ ] This week: Tumelo records 30-second voice sample → upload to Voicebox → test clone quality
