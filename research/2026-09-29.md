## TOPIC 1: SA B2B Coffee — Hotels, Restaurants & Offices

### Key Findings

- **Market momentum is real:** SA specialty coffee market is growing. Hostex 2026 report confirms HORECA operators are actively seeking differentiated suppliers — not just cheaper beans. Sustainability and provenance story are the two levers buyers care about most.
- **White-label bean supply is the foot-in-door:** Multiple boutique cafés (Bootlegger, Origin, Coffee Lab) are NOT roasting their own — they buy roasted beans. Volume is smaller than hotel chains but decision cycle is faster (1-2 weeks vs. procurement cycles).
- **Hotels = big volume, slow cycle:** The Saxon, Four Seasons, One&Only are high-volume targets but procurement is slow. Their pain point is **inconsistent quality across shifts** and **barista training gaps**. That's Studex's entry — not just beans, but a **managed coffee service with training SLA**.
- **O CS players (Dish Café, Vitago) are competitors AND partners:** They have the machine network but none offer barista training or cold brew. A bean-supply + training bundle deal with an OCS player could unlock 50+ locations overnight.
- **Cold brew is the trending product with highest margin:** RTD coffee market in SA is projected to hit $226M by 2033. Restaurants and hotels are adding cold brew to menus but have no reliable SA supplier. Cold brew concentrate supply = fast recurring revenue.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **Cold brew concentrate** — white-label to hotels/restaurants as a margin product. 5L concentrate bags, refill model.
2. **OCS partnership** — approach Dish Café or Vitago to supply premium beans to their managed service network. Training add-on differentiates.
3. **B2B landing page** (already built in `/workspace/sa-b2b-coffee-landing`) — needs traffic. Run it as a cold outreach + LinkedIn ads combo.

### Recommended Actions
- [ ] Contact 3 OCS companies (Dish Café, Vitago, Caffè Latte) this week with a bean supply proposal
- [ ] Price cold brew concentrate at 3x COGS for wholesale — target R180-R250/L for restaurants

## TOPIC 2: Agent Automation — Blotato, n8n, Make.com

### Key Findings

- **Blotato** is the most Studex-relevant tool already — it handles social media content generation + multi-platform posting (FB, IG, WhatsApp, Twitter, LinkedIn). The API key is live. The gap: no accounts connected yet. Blotato can generate content AND trigger via n8n/Make webhooks — so it fits INTO a larger workflow, not as a standalone.
- **n8n is the strategic winner for Studex.** It's open-source, self-hostable, and has native LangChain integration as of 2026. Can automate: AgentMail → GPT response → Notion log → Discord alert. Already a `/workspace/n8n` folder suggesting prior interest. Self-host on the Alibaba VM for full control.
- **Make.com** is more visual/non-technical but costs more and less flexible than n8n. Skip unless non-technical staff need to own workflows.
- **Higgsfield** is a video generation/playground tool — not relevant to Studex ops right now.
- **Agentic workflows are the shift:** The 2026 trend is AI agents that *do* tasks end-to-end, not just trigger single actions. n8n now wraps LangChain primitives (tools, memory, output parsers) — you can build a "Studex Order Agent" that checks Shopify → updates inventory → notifies WhatsApp → logs to Notion.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **Studex Order Agent** (n8n + AgentMail + Shopify): When an email comes in, AI reads it, extracts order, updates Shopify, confirms to customer — fully automated.
2. **Content-to-post pipeline** (Blotato + n8n): Blotato generates content → n8n triggers → posts to FB/IG/WhatsApp on schedule.
3. **Lead routing** (n8n): Form fills on Gulf landing page → categorize lead (hotels/restaurants/retail) → send to correct AgentMail inbox → CRM log.
