## TOPIC 2: B2B Coffee — Rwanda Trip Intel

### Key Findings

**Rwanda Coffee Market (Record Year)**
- Export revenues hit **$148.6M in 2025** (+53% surge). Average price **$6.20/kg** (+19% YoY)
- **$5.50–$6.50/lb FOB** for specialty fully washed Arabica — commands +155–200% premium over commodity price
- EU accounts for **~70% of Rwanda's market**; Belgium is historically the biggest buyer
- **China overtook DR Congo** as Rwanda's largest export destination — Asia is the fastest-growing buyer
- UAE is a growing hub but not Rwanda's primary buyer (Ethiopia leads UAE market share)
- **China has zero-tariff access** for African coffee under the AfCFTA — this is creating a structural buyer shift

**UAE/Dubai Buyer Requirements**
- Green coffee import duty: **0%** under GCC Common External Tariff (HS 0901.11)
- VAT: 5% at point of import
- Required: ESMA food safety compliance, Dubai Municipality food import registration, valid UAE trade license
- Dubai buyers at World of Coffee Dubai 2026 were specifically seeking: **distinctive flavour profiles, stronger brand presentation, closer supplier relationships** (shorter chains)

**EUDR Risk (December 2026)**
- EU Deforestation Regulation kicks in Dec 2026 — requires full traceability to farm level
- Rwanda is the **first African country with fully digital traceability** down to washing station level — this is a massive competitive advantage and a selling point Studex can use if sourcing Rwandan coffee

**SA B2B Coffee Buyer Pain Points (from prior research)**
- SA hotels/restaurants struggle with: inconsistent supply, lack of barista training support, no provenance story for their guests
- Differentiation opportunity: bundle coffee supply with biltong for corporate gifting — single supplier for premium hospitality hampers

### Opportunities for Studex
- **Tumelo in Rwanda (Aug 25):** Face-to-face meetings with washing station cooperatives and NAEB (Rwanda's coffee board) are extremely high-value — this is where supply chain relationships are built
- **UAE angle:** With 0% import duty and the Rwanda connection, Studex could position as a **Rwandan coffee + SA biltong bundle** supplier to UAE hospitality groups
- **Europe angle:** Belgian and German specialty roasters buy Rwanda — Studex could explore a white-label Rwandan roast for SA hotel groups who want "origin story" provenance on their menus

### Recommended Actions
1. **At Rwanda meeting (Aug 25+):** Ask NAEB specifically about UAE and China export partnerships — leverage Tumelo's visit to open these channels
2. Bring back 3 Rwanda coffee samples + contact details of 2 washing stations — use for SA hotel F&B pitch
3. Explore Dubai HORECA contacts — Ethiopia dominates UAE but Rwanda + SA biltong bundle is differentiated
