## TOPIC 1: South Africa B2B Coffee Market

### Key Findings

- **Market size:** SA coffee bean market valued at ~$650M (2025), growing at 4.79% CAGR through 2034. B2B segment is the dominant channel globally — hotels, restaurants, offices, and catering are the core buyers.
- **Specialty coffee surge:** More specialty cafés opening in SA major cities (JHB, CPT, DBN). Hostex 2026 (March) reinforced hospitality sector's shift toward premium/specialty positioning. B2B buyers want provenance, storytelling, and consistent quality — not just cheapest price.
- **Pricing:** Arabica trading at ~310–335 US cents/lb (~$3.10–3.35/lb) in late August 2026, down ~8.64% from prior month. Prices remain volatile — Q2 2026 saw sharp declines. Good window to lock in forward contracts.
- **EU deforestation regulation is a B2B barrier:** After Dec 30, 2026, coffee without a valid DDS (Due Diligence System) cannot clear EU customs. Penalty: up to 4% of annual turnover for the importer. If Studex ever sources SA-grown coffee for EU export, this is a blocker — but more importantly, EU B2B buyers will demand this documentation, which can become a *differentiator* for compliant African suppliers.
- **UAE opportunity:** UAE charges **zero customs duty on green coffee** (HS 0901), 5% VAT recoverable for registered businesses. This is a massive advantage — it means African-origin green coffee landed in UAE is effectively cheaper than competing origins. UAE is positioning as a re-export hub. This directly benefits Studex if they are trading commodity coffee into/through UAE.

### Opportunities for Studex
1. **B2B coffee supply contracts** — target 4-star+ hotels and corporate offices in JHB/CPT. Specialty single-origin at premium price, with DDS documentation for EU-facing clients.
2. **UAE as trade corridor** — investigate green bean sourcing through UAE (zero import duty). Could be a volume play alongside the existing retail line.
3. **Forward contracts now** — prices are down 8.6% MoM. Lock in Q4 stock at better margins. Coffee is a commodity; buy when cheap.
