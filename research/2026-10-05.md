## TOPIC 1: African Coffee in UAE/China/Europe — Market Intelligence

### Key Findings

- **China opened to ALL 53 African coffee origins — July 2026.** Massive new market access. Single continent-wide import policy eliminates bilateral deal complexity. Ethiopian, Kenyan, Rwandan, Tanzanian, and South African beans now have tariff-lite access to China's $42B coffee market.
- **China coffee market grew 150%+ in recent years.** 240,000+ metric tons consumed annually. Tier-1 cities (Shanghai, Beijing) driving premium single-origin demand. Green bean imports face lower barriers than roasted coffee (15–20% tariff on roasted).
- **UAE HoReCa is a proven buyer of African origins.** Dubai's specialty café scene actively sources Ethiopian Yirgacheffe and Kenyan AA. GCC drinks ~3.4 billion cups/year with 32% annual growth. 100% of coffee beans imported — no domestic production.
- **Europe requires sustainability certification.** Rainforest Alliance, Organic, Fair Trade certifications increasingly mandatory for EU trade. Ethiopia, Rwanda, Kenya already well-positioned. South Africa is a gap — no major EU-facing African coffee origin story yet.
- **MEA coffee market: $17.14B in 2026 → $24.89B by 2031 (7.75% CAGR).** Fastest-growing region globally. UAE and Saudi Arabia are the regional anchor buyers.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **South African coffee origin brand.** SA is not on the radar as a premium coffee origin. This is a positioning gap. "Roasted in Cape Town, sourced from African farms" — unique story for export.
2. **UAE export pathway.** Dubai luxury hotels + specialty cafés are the beachhead. Studex could supply whole bean + biltong snack pairing to hotel minibars/F&B.
3. **China green bean trading.** Low tariff entry point. SA roasters could buy green from Zambian/Zimbabwean farms via SA ports, roast locally, re-export to China.
4. **B2B coffee + luxury meat bundle.** Sell the bundle: Wagyu biltong + African single-origin coffee → premium hotel gift shop / executive gifting.
