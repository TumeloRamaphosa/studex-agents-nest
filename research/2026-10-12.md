## Topic 2: South Africa B2B Coffee Market

### Key Findings

- **B2B coffee is the dominant channel and growing at 4.9% CAGR through 2033.** Cafés, hotels, offices, and restaurants are the buyers. B2B generates more revenue than retail in South Africa. This is where volume is — not direct-to-consumer.

- **South Africa has 85 identified coffee suppliers (September 2026).** The market is active and competitive. Key players include Taste of Kenya/Rwanda specialty importers, large commercial roasters (Taste of Africa group, private label), and office coffee service (OCS) providers.

- **How hotels/restaurants/cafés source:** Procurement is done through (1) dedicated coffee distributors (NOT direct from farms for most buyers), (2) group purchasing organizations like Avendra (hospitality-specific, 5,000+ contracted suppliers), (3) annual tender process for large hotel groups. Lead time: 2-4 weeks for initial samples, 1-3 months for approved supplier status.

- **Pain points buyers face:** Price volatility (Arabica down 15% in 2026 but not always passed on), quality inconsistency between shipments, supply reliability (small specialty roasters fail to deliver on volume), and certification gaps (Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade, organic).

- **Huge opportunity: "Provenance + Certification + Cold-Chain" bundle.** SA hotel buyers want: (1) clear origin story for menu marketing, (2) all certifications in place, (3) reliable cold-chain (especially for green bean storage). Rwanda single-origin + Studex biltong bundled pitch hits all three.

- **CIIE angle is also a China AND SA story.** After CIIE, SA hotel groups will see the Studex brand. Use CIIE momentum to pitch SA luxury hotels directly: "As served at CIIE China — Rwandan single-origin coffee + African biltong for your F&B program."

- **Office coffee service (OCS) in SA:** Companies like Coffee Machine Solutions, Fresh Coffee, and others service corporate offices. They buy green beans or roasted bags. Studex white-label coffee bags (250g/500g/1kg branded) could be an OCS supplier add-on — lower MOQ than a hotel tender.

### Opportunities for Studex

- **SA hotel outreach after CIIE.** Use the CIIE sample box + zero-tariff momentum to pitch SA luxury hotels directly. Target: Cape Town hotels, Sandton business hotels, Kruger lodges. Rwanda coffee + biltong as welcome amenity = easy pilot.

- **White-label coffee bags for OCS market.** Smaller MOQ (24 units) for office coffee service suppliers. Branded Rwanda origin. Can be a faster revenue line while the hotel tender process runs (3-6 months).

- **Certification is the unlock for hotel procurement.** Get Rainforest Alliance or Fairtrade certification for Rwanda coffee. Without it, many hotel groups won't get past stage 1 of procurement. Blotato can publish the certification announcement.
