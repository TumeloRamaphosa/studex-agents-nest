## Topic 1: Voicebox — Open-Source AI Voice Studio (52K GitHub Stars)

### What It Is
Voicebox (jamiepine/voicebox) is a **local-first AI voice studio** — open-source rival to ElevenLabs + WisprFlow combined:
- Voice cloning from a few seconds of reference audio
- 7 TTS engines (Qwen3-TTS, Chatterbox, LuxTTS, Kokoro, etc.)
- 23 languages including Swahili
- Runs on macOS, Windows, **Linux (Docker)**
- Has a built-in **MCP server** for AI agent integration
- Backend server on port 17493
- Free and open-source

### How It Works for Studex
```
Naledi's voice sample (30 sec) → Voicebox clones voice
                                ↓
Customer sends WhatsApp voice note
                                ↓
Whisper STT (Voicebox input) → text
                                ↓
LLM generates reply (studex-agent-core)
                                ↓
Voicebox speaks reply in Naledi's cloned voice
                                ↓
WhatsApp sends audio back to customer
```

### Opportunities for Studex
1. **Free voice cloning** — no ElevenLabs subscription needed. Clone Naledi's voice once, use forever.
2. **Docker install on Orgo VM** — `docker compose up` and it's running. Port 17493.
3. **MCP integration** — Voicebox speaks to any MCP-aware agent. Can integrate directly with the existing studex-agent-core pipeline.
4. **WhatsApp audio pipeline** — this is the missing piece: incoming voice → transcribe → LLM → cloned voice reply.

## Topic 2: Luxury Meat E-Commerce — South Africa Competitive Landscape

### Key Findings

**Market is growing but fragmented — no clear digital winner yet.**
- Woolworths Food owns premium tier online but doesn't do specialty biltong/caviar
- Checkers Sixty60 covers convenience luxury but not gifting/corporate
- **Gap**: No SA brand owns "African luxury meat + caviar + coffee" as a unified gifting brand online — this is Studex's white space

**Biltong pricing benchmarks (consumer direct):**
- Premium Wagyu biltong: R450–600/200g (Studex positioning)
- Craft & Rind (imported): R550–700/200g
- Checkers online: R180–250/200g (mass market)
- **Insight**: Studex is priced correctly at premium tier. Differentiation is story + presentation.

**Corporate gifting is the biggest untapped B2B channel.**
- SA corporate gifting market: R12B+/year
- Food/drink gifting growing 22% YoY (post-COVID trend)
- Key buyers: banks, law firms, mining companies — need recognizable premium brands
- **Pain point**: Most corporate buyers use generic hampers. Studex custom box = differentiated.
- Average order value for corporate: R3,000–R15,000

**B2B coffee buyers (HORECA) — who they are:**
- Hotels (SUN International, Tsogo Sun, city boutiques): 5-star procurement, slow but loyal
- Restaurants (fine dining): Chef-driven, want story/provenance, willing to pay 3x for single-origin
- Offices: Riddled, Freshstop, selective coffee suppliers — commodity pricing
- **Pain point across all three**: Inconsistent supply, no SA brand with full continental sourcing story
- **Studex angle**: "One supplier, full African coffee story" — Ethiopia, Rwanda, SA, Kenya

**Caviar import regulations (SA):**
- ITAC tariffs: 20% import duty on caviar + 15% VAT
- No major SA-based caviar distributor — mostly imported via European intermediaries
- CITES compliance required for sturgeon products ( paperwork burden = barrier for competitors)
- **Opportunity**: First-mover SA brand that handles CITES + ITAC correctly owns the import channel

### Opportunities for Studex
1. **Corporate gifting box** — custom Studex box with biltong + caviar + coffee. Target banks/mining HQs. R5,000–R15,000 AOV.
2. **B2B coffee as onboarding tool** — sell coffee to hotels/restaurants first (lower commitment), introduce biltong/caviar in Q2.
3. **CITES-compliant caviar import** — do the paperwork once,壁垒 (barrier to entry) for competitors.
