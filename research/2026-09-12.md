## 1. Voicebox — AI Voice Agent for Customer Service

### What It Is
- **Repo:** github.com/jamiepine/voicebox
- **Creator:** Jamie Pine (Supacode founder — known for Gank and other developer tools)
- **Type:** Open-source AI voice conversation framework
- **What it does:** Real-time voice AI agent that can answer calls, have natural conversations, handle bookings/complaints/inquiries — fully programmable

### Key Capabilities
- Connects to Twilio, phone lines, and webRTC
- Real-time speech-to-text + LLM + text-to-speech pipeline
- Programmable flows, no-code-ish with code control
- Multi-language support — relevant for Studex WhatsApp + future voice channel

## 2. South Africa B2B Coffee Market

### Who the Buyers Are
- **Hotels (4-5 star):** Tourists + corporate events. Source via food service distributors (Bidfood, Foodcorp, CheckersWholesale). Care about: consistency, origin story, espresso quality. Willing to pay premium.
- **Restaurants & Cafés:** Growing specialty coffee culture in JHB, CPT, DBN. Source direct from roasters OR use distributors. Care about: flavor profiles, training support, equipment loans.
- **Offices (corporate):** Refreshment + hospitality buyers. Source via coffee service companies (Clover, Nestlé Professional, Rhodes Food Group). Pain point: boring, generic coffee. Opportunity: premium single-origin with branding.
- **Retail (spaza → boutique):** Informal market + premium grocery (Woolworths, Spar). Complex logistics, low margins in informal.

### Pain Points (What They Hate)
1. **Inconsistent quality** — roasters can't guarantee supply
2. **Slow procurement** — long lead times for specialty needs
3. **No brand story** — buyers want to sell a narrative, not just beans
4. **Equipment lock-in** — service companies force their machines
5. **Import delays** — port congestion, customs (Durban, Cape Town)

### Price Benchmarks (2026 est.)
| Segment | Price/kg (ZAR) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial | R80–120 | Nestlé, instant dominant |
| Premium | R200–400 | Single-origin, specialty |
| Ultra-premium | R500–900 | Microlots, competition grade |

### Opportunities for Studex Coffee
- **B2B supply contracts** with hotels/restaurants: bulk orders, exclusive origin story
- **Private label** for offices: white-label premium blend in Studex black/gold packaging
- **Export to UAE/China:** African coffee has strong cachet there — Studex already has trade infrastructure
- **Subscription model:** Monthly single-origin deliveries to high-end offices
