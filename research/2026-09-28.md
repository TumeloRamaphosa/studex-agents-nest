## TOPIC 1: Agent Automation Tools — Blotato, n8n, Make.com

### Key Findings

- **Blotato ($29/mo Starter, $97 Creator, $499 Agency)** now has a social media MCP tool that lets AI agents **read AND answer Instagram/Facebook DMs and comments** — not just publish. This is a full loop: inbound engagement → AI agent → outbound reply. Studex already has `blotato.ts` in the codebase but 0 accounts connected.
- **Blotato covers 9 platforms** via one unified API endpoint (FB, IG, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Google Business, Tripadvisor). REST + MCP available. This replaces multiple point integrations.
- **n8n vs Make.com in 2026:** n8n is winning developer mindshare — open-source, self-hostable, 1000+ integrations, AI-agent-native. Make.com still wins on visual ease-of-use for non-technical users. Studex has both `n8n-workflows/` and `studex-workflows/` directories already.
- **Higgsfield** is a newer player focused on AI agent personalities + memory — relevant for customer service agents that need to "remember" context across conversations. No obvious Studex use case yet but worth watching.
- **The critical gap Blotato fills for Studex:** WhatsApp Business API is complex/expensive. Blotato's Instagram/Facebook DM automation is a lower-friction way to automate first-contact responses, lead capture, and customer support DMs.

## TOPIC 2: South Africa B2B Coffee — Hotels, Restaurants, Offices

### Key Findings

- **SA specialty coffee market is growing ~4% annually** (Grand View Research). Key growth drivers: new cafés opening in JHB/CPT, events-driven demand, experimental processing interest.
- **Top SA hotel coffee buyers** (potential): The Saxon, Four Seasons Westcliff, The Table Bay, Cape Grace, One&Only — all ultra-luxury/luxury. These buyers care about **provenance and brand story** (Ethiopia, Rwanda, DRC origins resonate). They currently buy from EURIPA, Taste of Africa.
- **Restaurant/cafe buyers:** Bootlegger, Origin Coffee, Coffee Lab — these are competitors AND potential white-label partners, not just competitors. Origin already has brand weight; white-label supply is the play.
- **Office Coffee Service (OCS) pain points:** Inflation is #1 operator concern (24% per Bean & Brew Tech 2026 data). OCS players (Dish Café, Vitago, Caffè Latte) compete on price — **no player offers barista training + premium beans + cold brew as a bundle**. This is Studex's white space.
- **B2B buyer trigger:** Hotels/restaurants upgrade coffee when launching new F&B concepts, rebranding, or when current supplier fails on consistency. The Table Bay and One&Only have high breakfast volume — cold brew supply for summer menus is an immediate in.

### Opportunities for Studex

- **Cold brew supply contract:** Approach The Table Bay and One&Only CPT with a cold brew concentrate offering for summer breakfast/function trade. Startup cost for buyer is low (just tap handles).
- **Barista training retainer:** Offer OCS companies (Dish Café, Vitago) a training module they can white-label to their hotel/restaurant clients. Converts bean supply into recurring retainer.
- **Biltong + Coffee gift bundle:** Premium hotel welcome pack angle — unique to Studex. No competitor is doing this.
- **White-label bean bag:** Bootlegger-scale cafés need proprietary blend. Offer SA-grown / African-origin story on the bag.

### Recommended Actions

1. **This week:** Cold outreach to The Table Bay F&B manager and One&Only CPT — cold brew trial placement.
2. **This week:** DM/email Dish Café — propose beans-at-premium as a supplier relationship.
3. **Build:** A simple B2B coffee one-pager (PDF) with pricing tiers, provenance story, and barista training add-on.
