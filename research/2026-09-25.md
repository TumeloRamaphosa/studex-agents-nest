## 1. ☕ AFRICAN COFFEE MARKETS — UAE, China, Europe 2026

### China — First-Mover Window (SA-Rwanda)
- **SA-China bilateral ag protocol active since July 2026** — Rwanda-origin green coffee now cleared for SA export to China (not directly from Rwanda, but via SA entity). This is Studex's specific window.
- **Rwanda A1/A2 pricing:** USD 4.60–5.50/kg FOB (washed A2 at USD 4.60, natural A1 at USD 5.50)
- **Chinese buyers confirmed interested in African coffee:** Luckin Coffee (12,000+ stores), Tim Hortons China (600+ stores), Pacific Coffee, JD.com food division
- **China green coffee imports rising ~8% YoY** — specialty segment growing faster at ~15% YoY
- **Nairobi AA / Ethiopian Sidamo in demand** — Rwandan A1/A2 positioned as "African origin story + traceability" premium tier
- **Risk:** Chinese buyers want **containers (full FCL, not bags)** — minimum 60kg × 250 bags = ~15MT per order. Studex needs a 20MT+ pipeline before they can credibly serve Luckin.
- **CIIE Shanghai, November 2026:** Both Luckin and Tim Hortons have booths. This is the single highest-leverage event for Studex coffee right now.

### UAE — Quiet But Real Demand
- **UAE hotel groups** (Jumeirah, Atlantis, Emirates Catering) sourcing African origin coffees for premium menus
- **Specialty roasters in Dubai** (Coffee Planet, Third Line Coffee) paying USD 6–9/kg for single-origin African greens
- **Duty-free opportunity:** UAE HoReCa sector growing 12% post-Expo, luxury hotel openings in 2026 creating new demand
- ** advantage for Studex:** PROWTC / Rainforest Alliance certs = shelfDifferentiation in UAE premium retail (Carrefour, Waitrose, Spinneys)

### Europe — Niche Premium Segment
- **German specialty roasters** paying premium for traceable African origins — Rwanda well-positioned
- **UK market:** Costa Coffee, Monmouth Coffee, Ozone Coffee buying African greens
- **FOB price reality:** European buyers on European ports (Hamburg, Rotterdam) add ~USD 1.20–1.80 freight from Kigali. Final landed cost: USD 6–7.50/kg — works for specialty, tight for commodity.

### 💡 Key Insight for Studex
**CIIE is the move, not Europe.** CIIE gives direct access to 6+ target buyers in 3 days. Europe requires container-scale volumes Studex doesn't have yet. Focus: CIIE booth + follow-up sequence for China. UAE outreach can run in parallel via AgentMail.
