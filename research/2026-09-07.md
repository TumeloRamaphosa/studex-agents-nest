## 🎯 TOPIC 2: SA B2B COFFEE — HORECA LANDSCAPE & PAIN POINTS

### Key Findings

**Market Size (2026)**
- SA coffee market: 6.2% CAGR (from Jan 2026 market data)
- Horeca (hotels, restaurants, cafes, offices) = largest B2B buyer segment
- Specialty/coffee shop segment growing fastest — consumers trading up, not down in premium coffee

**How SA Horeca Buyers Source (from existing prospects file + fresh intel)**
- **Hotels**: Procurement departments run RFPs for bean supply contracts (annual). Decision makers: F&B Manager, Executive Chef, GM. Lead time: 3-6 months. Contracts are sticky — once you're in, you're in for 12-24 months
- **Restaurants/cafes**: Owner-operated places buy from specialty roasters or direct from importers. Faster decision cycles (weeks, not months). Relationship-driven
- **Offices (OCS providers)**: Companies like Dish Café, Vitago, Caffè Latte dominate. They rent machines + supply beans. These players are **both competitors and partners** — they have the machine network, Studex has the premium bean story

**Key Pain Points SA Horeca Buyers Face**
1. **Supply consistency** — "Can you deliver 50kg/month every month without fail?" This is the #1 filter
2. **Certification traceability** — Rainforest Alliance / sustainability certs increasingly required by luxury hotels. International brands (Four Seasons, Radisson) have global standards
3. **Delivery reliability** — Cold chain and lead time are a problem in SA logistics
4. **Barista quality** — Mid-market hotels and restaurants struggle with inconsistent coffee because staff can't pull a proper shot. Training is a value-add most suppliers don't offer
5. **Price pressure** — Coffee inflation is real. Buyers squeezing margins, looking for direct-trade efficiency
