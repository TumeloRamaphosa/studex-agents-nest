## Topic 1: African Coffee — UAE, China, Europe Market

### Key Findings
- **China opened market to ALL 53 African countries for coffee imports** (July 2026) — removed country-by-country quarantine requirements. Q1 2025 African coffee imports to China grew **+70.4% YoY**. This is massive. China's middle class is waking up to African single-origin coffee.
- **Global coffee market: $489.1B in 2026 → $657.8B by 2035** (CAGR ~3.4%). Prices at decade highs. Arabica premiums widening — quality African origins in demand.
- **UAE coffee beans market: $0.72B** — strong café/hospitality sector, re-export hub between Africa and Asia. Dubai is positioning as the halal/specialty coffee gateway.
- **Europe still dominates global consumption** (32.36% market share) but growth plateauing. Premium/specialty segment growing, commodity segment flat. Africa-sourced specialty going to Europe but competition from Ethiopia/Kenya/Yimbabwe is fierce.
- **Sustainability certification (Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade) is now table stakes** for European and UAE hotel buyers. SA coffee without certification is losing shelf space.

### Opportunities for Studex ☕
- **China is the goldmine right now.** Ethiopean and Kenyan beans already favored — SA has geographic advantage with faster shipping. A Studex China desk (even informal) could place SA roasters into Chinese café chains.
- **UAE as re-export hub:** Dubai buyers actively sourcing African origins. Studex should attend or exhibit at a Dubai hospitality trade show in 2026.
- **Pricing:** Current high prices benefit producers. Lock in long-term supply contracts with SA roasters NOW before prices potentially correct.

## Topic 2: South Africa B2B Coffee — Hotels, Restaurants, Offices

### Key Findings
- **6 million SA consumers** buying coffee regularly. Out-of-home (OOH) channel growing — at-work, hospitality, forecourts.
- **Load shedding is a hidden B2B pain point.** Coffee shops and offices using it as a **reason to switch suppliers** to those offering reliable equipment + backup solutions.
- **Specialty coffee culture booming in major metros** (JHB, CPT, DBN). Demand for single-origin, locally roasted. But **supply chain vulnerability** is the #1 buyer fear — global coffee prices fluctuating, SA imports most of its coffee.
- **Big players (Taste of Africa, Coffee Junction, KZN Coffee)** competing hard on B2B contracts. Long-term supply agreements with hotels/office groups are the prize. Volume + margin pressure is real.
- **Smaller boutique roasters winning on story + certification.** Hotels want a "South African story" on their coffee menu. This is Studex's adjacency to caviar and premium meat — the brand story is already there.
