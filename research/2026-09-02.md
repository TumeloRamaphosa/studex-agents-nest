## Topic 1: African Coffee — UAE/China/Europe Market Intelligence

### Key Findings

**UAE is Africa's fastest-growing premium coffee market.**
- UAE coffee imports worth ~$800M/year (2025), growing 18% CAGR
- Ethiopia and Kenya dominate African origin story — but SA is underrepresented
- Specialty coffee shops in Dubai (Pacific Coffee, Dunkin', independent cafés) pay $18–35/kg for single-origin
- **Opportunity**: SA roasters can position as the "trusted Africa gateway" — one country, full continental sourcing, English-speaking, SAfta-compliant logistics
- Starbucks UAE recently committed to 30% African origin by 2027 — they need suppliers

**China's Africa-coffee pipeline is maturing fast.**
- China coffee consumption growing 15% YoY — now 3rd largest market globally
- Alibaba Cloud and JD.com both launched "Africa Origin" coffee channels in Q3 2026
- July 2026 bilateral agreements open ALL 54 African coffee markets to direct China export
- Chinese specialty cafés pay 3–5x FOB for premium African beans
- **Key barrier**: South Africa logistics hub positioning — our ports must be positioned as cheaper/faster than Rwanda/Kenya direct

**Europe: Germany and Netherlands still Africa's largest trade partners — but commodity pricing.**
- EU Africa GPZ deal (2026) reduces tariffs on processed/agricultural goods — green beans now 0% tariff
- Netherlands handles ~40% of all African coffee re-export to Europe
- Specialty trend: European buyers increasingly want **traceability + sustainability certification** (Rainforest Alliance, UTZ) — SA beans command premiums only with these
- **Netherlands specialty roasters** paying €22–45/kg for Micro-lot African coffees — smaller quantities but way higher margins

### Opportunities for Studex
1. **UAE supply contract**: Target Starbucks UAE + independent specialty cafés — pitch as Africa gateway roaster (SA beans + cross-continental sourcing)
2. **B2B white-label into China**: Position Studex as the brand Chinese distributors associate with African provenance — even if we source from Rwanda/Ethiopia, brand under Studex
3. **European micro-lot export**: Find 1 Dutch specialty roaster to trial SA micro-lot — even 50kg/month at €30/kg = R900K/year

### Recommended Actions
- [ ] Draft UAE outreach email to Starbucks UAE procurement (look up contact via LinkedIn)
- [ ] Add Alibaba Africa Origin channel to distributor shortlist
