- **Price expectation**: European buyers expect USD 4.50–6.00/kg for standard grades. Studex Rwanda pricing is within range.
- **Challenge**: Competing with Tanzania, Ethiopia, Uganda which all have established European trade lanes. Need a differentiated story.
