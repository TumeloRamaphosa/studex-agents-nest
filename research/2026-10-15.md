## Topic 1: SA HORECA Coffee Market

### Key Findings

- **SA imports most of its coffee** — local supply chains are exposed to global FX and logistics volatility. Any SA roaster or distributor with reliable supply and competitive pricing has a structural advantage.
- **Specialty coffee is the growth vector.** The PDGrind March 2026 report confirms SA's specialty coffee market is expanding — more cafés opening in major cities, more events driving consumer interest. The action is in the R150–R400/kg specialty tier, not commodity.
- **83 tracked coffee roasters in SA** (RevenueBase, Sep 2026). Fragmented market — no single dominant player in B2B supply. Gap for a well-branded B2B coffee company.
- **HORECA segment is the fastest-growing segment** in the coffee roaster market globally (5.94% CAGR through 2031). Hotels, restaurants, and catering are volume buyers with recurring monthly orders.
- **White-label coffee is a low-risk entry play.** Beanco Coffee (SA) offers white-label/private-label with NO minimum order quantity. Studex could launch a "Wildlife Coffee" B2B brand without owning a roastery — Beanco roasts, blends, packages under Studex branding.
- **Private label B2B coffee margins:** Roasters typically charge 20–35% margin on white label. A Studex-branded B2B coffee line sold to SA hotels/offices could generate R80–R150/kg margin. At 50kg/month per client, that's R4,000–R7,500/month per account.
- **Corporate offices are underserved for premium coffee.** Most SA offices use instant coffee or cheap bean. A premium B2B coffee subscription (delivered monthly, equipment loaned free) targeting JHB/CPT corporate offices is a proven model in EU/UK.

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **Launch Wildlife Coffee white-label B2B line** — partner with Beanco (no MOQ), brand as "Wildlife Coffee by Studex," target SA hotels and restaurants. One page website, WhatsApp ordering, monthly delivery.
2. **Corporate office subscription** — "Wildlife Coffee for your team." Equipment loan (brewer/expresso machine), monthly 5kg bag delivery. Price: R1,200–R1,800/month per office. 20 offices = R24,000–R36,000/month recurring.
3. **CIIE / Kigali angle** — international buyers attending these events already buy coffee. Studex Wildlife Coffee could be a loss-leader add-on to the meat pitch: "We also supply premium SA-roasted coffee to offices across Africa."

## Topic 2: Voicebox — Open-Source AI Voice Studio

### What It Is

Voicebox (github.com/jamiepine/voicebox) is a **local-first, open-source AI voice studio** — free alternative to ElevenLabs and Wispr Flow. Key capabilities:
- **Voice cloning** from a few seconds of audio
- **Multi-track audio composition** (voice + music + SFX)
- **7 TTS engines** including ElevenLabs-equivalent quality
- **Chatterbox Multilingual engine** — zero-shot voice cloning in **23 languages** including English, Arabic, Mandarin, French (Feb 2026 update)
- **Self-hostable** — runs locally, no cloud subscription required
- **Free** — open source, no per-character costs

### Relevance to Studex

1. **WhatsApp AI customer service** — In 2026, WhatsApp AI agents autonomously resolve 76–92% of customer interactions, respond in <3 seconds, cost 12× less than human agents. A well-built WhatsApp concierge could solve the Shopify blind-spot problem: if orders come in via WhatsApp (which Studex's business account does), an AI agent can parse orders, check inventory, and reply with quotes — without needing Shopify API.
2. **Voice-based ordering** — Voicebox can clone Tumelo's voice. A customer sends a WhatsApp voice note → Whisper transcription → GPT response → Tumelo's cloned voice reply. This is a genuinely differentiated customer experience no competitor in SA meat/coffee is doing.
3. **Arabic language support** — Chatterbox engine supports Arabic. Since Studex serves UAE/diaspora customers, a WhatsApp agent that can reply in Arabic is a real competitive edge.
4. **Content generation** — Voicebox can generate audio ads, product narration, social media voiceovers. Studex could produce Arabic + English audio ads for Meta at essentially zero cost.

### What It Cannot Do (Yet)

- Voicebox is a local TTS/voice cloning tool — not a full AI agent by itself
- Needs to be combined with: OpenAI Whisper (transcription) + GPT-4o (reasoning) + WhatsApp Business API (delivery)
- This is a "build vs. buy" decision — self-hosted gives full control, but requires technical setup

### Alternatives Considered

| Platform | Cost | Arabic | Self-hosted | SA WhatsApp ready |
|----------|------|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Voicebox** | Free (self-hosted) | ✅ 23 langs | ✅ | ⚠️ Needs agent stack |
| **ElevenLabs** | $0.08–$1.32/min | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ TTS only |
| **WATI / Respond.io** | R800–R2,500/mo | ✅ | ❌ SaaS | ✅ All-in-one |
| **n8n + WhatsApp API** | Self-hosted | ✅ via GPT | ✅ | ✅ Full control |

### Opportunities for Studex

1. **WhatsApp AI concierge (low-cost build)** — self-hosted Voicebox + n8n + WhatsApp Business API. When Shopify API is restored, sync orders. Until then, WhatsApp takes orders manually routed.
2. **Voice-first customer experience** — Clone Tumelo's voice + Arabic voice for UAE customers. WhatsApp voice notes from customers get AI responses in the customer's language with Studex's actual voice.
3. **Content production at zero marginal cost** — Arabic + English audio ads, product narration for Instagram Reels, WhatsApp status audio updates.
