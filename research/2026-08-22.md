## TOPIC 2: SA B2B Coffee — Hotels, Restaurants, Offices

### Key Findings

**Market Size & Growth**
- SA coffee market: R3.9B (2020) → R8.1B (2025) — CAGR ~15.7%, fastest-growing food category segment
- Specialty/single-origin now the dominant growth driver — commodity robusta declining
- SA is a **net importer** of coffee — highly exposed to FX fluctuations and global supply shocks

**Buyer Pain Points (Hotels/Restaurants/Offices)**
| Pain Point | Detail |
|---|---|
| Load shedding | Disrupts espresso machines, coffee quality consistency — major operational risk |
| Green coffee prices | Up due to lower global yields (climate impact on Brazil, Vietnam crops) |
| Supply inconsistency | Import dependency = variable supply, FX-dependent pricing |
| Guest expectations | Now demand specialty café-level quality in-room and at conference venues |
| Ethical sourcing | Buyers expect: Rainforest Alliance cert, compostable packaging, full traceability |
| No provenance story | Most SA hotels serve generic coffee — zero marketing angle for their guests |

**What Hotel/Restaurant Buyers Want (2026)**
- Single-origin beans with **narrative** (origin farm story = Instagram content for the property)
- Barista-level quality at scale — consistent across all outlets
- Sustainability credentials — EUDR-ready (full farm traceability by Dec 2026)
- **Bundle opportunity:** Coffee supply + biltong corporate gifting = single premium supplier story

- **Studex angle:** Rwandan coffee origin story + SA Wagyu biltong = dual premium product bundle for UAE hotel groups. No competitor is doing this.
